# Yemen domestic candidate — initial research, 2026-09-26

**Research started; no local statistical edition accepted or published.** The separate ignored `generated/yemen-areadata-20260926` was produced by `node scripts/create-country.mjs --country Yemen --out generated/yemen-areadata-20260926`. It has 268 national WDI observations and 22 geoBoundaries 2017 reference ADM1 units only. These are neither official matched local geography nor Yemen domestic observations.

See [official source-location evidence](../../evidence/yemen-source-location-2026-09-26.md). The CSO 2004 census report URL was identified, but direct acquisition returned non-PDF content. The CSO governorate yearbook catalogue was located without acquiring the individual volumes. A 40-page Parliament Local Authority Law No. 4 (2000) PDF was acquired and hashed, while current application, later amendments, administrative unit correspondence, planning guidance and actual plan/budget/report/evaluation files remain unverified. A law title or old text does not prove a selected district has a current plan.

The private `raw/official-yemen/acquisition.json` records the different retrieval outcomes. `scripts/collect-yemen-official-leads.py` replays those downloads, and `scripts/register-yemen-source-locations.py` adds leads to the candidate source register and preflight without inventing local numbers. Country validation has zero errors and two warnings: national-only statistics and no verified planning documents. Local site build succeeds; this is a bootstrap, not domestic acceptance. `evidence/SOURCE_PREFLIGHT.md` and `.json` are the first resume points. The next pass should acquire an authentic current CSO census original, inventory all available tables and yearbooks, verify official governorate/district codes and boundary epochs, then build source-specific adapters and inspect real local plans.
