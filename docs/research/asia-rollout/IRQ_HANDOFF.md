# Iraq domestic candidate — handoff, 2026-09-26

**Status: partial census-based domestic candidate; not independently accepted or published.** The existing international statistics portfolio is reused. It is not a substitute for the domestic 2024 census.

- The official [government open-data record](https://nogp.gov.iq/DatasetDetails.aspx?id=17) supplied `تعداد 2024.xlsx` through the public page's download action. The direct API resource endpoint failed, though [dataset metadata](https://nogp.gov.iq/api/v1/datasets/17) listed the same resource. Local raw workbook SHA-256 `2f2446396744ec8c760b797d46b2b177ec8954aa2ef8542c5c319f9b608176f9`, 13,294 bytes. Original bytes and receipt are ignored by Git. The portal envelope says CC BY 4.0; resource-specific terms are still being checked.
- `scripts/import-iraq-census-2024.py` checks the source hash, sheet layout, 18 distinct governorate names, four additive national totals and the relationship adjusted population minus foreigners = adjusted Iraqi population. It imports five indicators and 95 observations for the country and all 18 governorates. The original tabulation and Cabinet Decision 24853/2024 adjustment remain separate concepts. The national total in both population columns is 46,118,793.
- The official Arabic governorate labels were manually paired with 18 geoBoundaries 2022 reference names. This is name correspondence for navigation only; no official geography code or 2024 legal boundary equivalence is proven. The comparison map cannot be described as a census-certified boundary.
- The [COSIT final 2024 census report](https://cosit.gov.iq/ar/1234-2019-09-05-07-53-20) was acquired as a 36,060,920-byte, 562-page PDF; SHA-256 `0f3bba10ad79dbc3c6add02c67f55d76363f9be52417e46cb72046e1565e5aa7`. Its detailed tables and fields are **not** inventoried or adopted. PDF text extraction is not reliable enough yet for cell-level claims. Do not infer district coverage from acquisition alone.
- Country validation has zero errors and one warning for no verified planning documents; the site builds locally. Browser checked the national 2024 count and Baghdad 9,780,429, parent return, map/navigation and 18/18 governorate comparisons. The diagnostic CSV/HTML and planning HTML/evidence CSV for country, Baghdad and Al-Basrah were generated and checked for value, period, source URL and full comparison row count by `scripts/verify-asia-domestic-partials.mjs`; hashes and first/last rows reside in ignored `evidence/OUTPUT_VERIFICATION.json`. Planning output is generic because no official Iraqi plan is adopted. Independent audit, mobile, plan contents and hosting remain unverified.
- The selected domestic governorate diagnosis hides national-only WDI indicator cards while preserving those series in the research database, country page and exports. A selected 2024 census value is never filled from a national estimate.
