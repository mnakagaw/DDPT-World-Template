# Iran AreaData — handoff, 2026-09-26

## Status

The first Middle East work-group country is a **partial census-based domestic candidate**, not an accepted country edition. The 2016 SCI-authored census workbook copies were imported into the AreaData local project: 31 ostans, 429 shahrestans, 49 census indicators and 4,571 census observations. Planning documents, official local codes, and verified boundaries remain absent. It is not connected to the Asia portal, independently audited, or published. The existing world international-statistics portfolio is reused rather than recollected here.

## Source evidence checked

- `evidence/SOURCE_PREFLIGHT.md` reports that official country-specific source locations were not yet pre-researched. Its cross-country candidates have not been checked for Iran and must not be treated as acquisitions.
- The UNSD census listing in `data/census/world-census-listings-v1.8.1.json` names the Statistical Centre of Iran and lists the 2016 population and housing census (24 September–20 November 2016); its catalogue entry has no direct original link and no acquired data.
- A [UNSD metadata response supplied by Iran](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/metadata/Asia/PE/Iran-2019-PE-Metadata.pdf) identifies the 2016 census as the base for its 2019 population-estimate report and points to the Statistical Centre's census pages. The [official census page URL](https://www.amar.org.ir/english/Population-and-Housing-Censuses/Census-2016-General-Results) could not be fetched in this session. Source location is a lead; original tables, variables, and current availability remain unverified.
- [Iran Data Portal's 2016 census catalogue](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/census/census-2016.) distributes the SCI-authored [province/shahrestan population and household workbook](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/wp-content/uploads/Population-and-Households.xlsx) (`SHA-256 beaa06950520e65892959c6dabe9013284907b4a49fc9ac89485c8800b0f60b3`) and [province age/sex workbook](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/wp-content/uploads/Population-by-Age-and-Sex-provincial-level.xlsx) (`SHA-256 b3154c61e73c58ff61b3452644a49d9d2534dfc7ce11a4eb3a2773bdacc07556`). The local project retains the original bytes and receipts outside Git. `scripts/import-iran-census-2016.py` asserts both hashes and preserves sheet/cell locators. This is a university-distributed copy, not a successful replay from the official SCI host; redistribution terms remain under review.
- The population sheet has one national block, 31 ostan blocks and 429 shahrestan blocks. Every province total equals the sum of its counties for population, male, female and households; the 31 provinces equal the national totals (79,926,270 people, 40,498,442 male, 39,427,828 female, 24,196,035 households). Urban/rural/unsettled components and age/sex bands also balance. The population sheet labels the 768,898-person province `Khorasan-e-Razavi`, while the age/sex sheet calls it `South Khorasan`. The age/sex sheet labels the 3,164,718-person province `Kermanshah`, while the population sheet calls it `Kerman`. These source-label conflicts are retained in `evidence/IRN_CENSUS_IMPORT_AUDIT.json`; adopted labels come from the other matching census table, using unique population counts and row positions, rather than a silent name-only join.
- `raw/geoboundaries-adm1-metadata.json` reports 33 ADM1 features in a 2017 reference boundary, while its downloaded simplified geometry has 32 features. The shapes include two `IR-21 Mazandaran` features and a Tehran feature without an ISO subdivision code. The generator correctly adopted 0 ADM1 units after the count mismatch. This is a reference boundary lead, not a verified official administrative-code join.

## Actual local verification

- `node scripts/validate-country.mjs --project generated/iran-areadata-20260926`: 0 errors; one warning for missing verified planning documents. `build-country.mjs` rebuilt the local site.
- Local browser: the 2016 territorial view showed Tehran ostan 13,267,637 and Eslamshahr shahrestan 548,620. Selecting the Tehran parent again cleared the Eslamshahr selection and restored the province URL and value. The thematic page showed 31/31 provinces, then 16/16 Tehran counties, for the 2016 census population indicator. The country dataset now defaults to `latest-available` for each indicator; an explicitly selected 2025 WDI period retains that specified year and leaves 2016 census values missing until changed.
- Local maps state that no verified boundary exists; no substitute polygon is shown. The source-row IDs are provisional and are not official administrative codes. The national, Tehran and Eslamshahr diagnostic CSV/HTML and planning HTML/evidence CSV were generated and their census population values, source URL, comparison row counts, first/last rows and hashes recorded in `evidence/OUTPUT_VERIFICATION.json`. This is a local output check, not independent acceptance; no official planning document is adopted.
