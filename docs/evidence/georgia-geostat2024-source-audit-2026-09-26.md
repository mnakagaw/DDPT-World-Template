# Georgia Geostat final 2024 census source audit — 2026-09-26

Scope: **partial, unpublished** candidate in ignored `generated/georgia-areadata-20260926`. The generated `SOURCE_PREFLIGHT.md` and `.json` had no Georgia-specific domestic source research; the ten common international leads were not treated as available local statistics. The [Geostat final 2024 census catalogue](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/12/2024-population-census-of-georgia-results), [final overview](https://www.geostat.ge/media/78282/2024-Population-and-Agricultural-Census-of-Georgia-Finalized-results.pdf) and [June 2026 detailed-results announcement](https://www.geostat.ge/en/single-news/3790/main-results-of-the-2024-population-and-agricultural-census) distinguish the final 14 November 2024 census from its preliminary count and subsequent population estimates. Its **3,929,581** usual residents exclude occupied territories. The 2024 WDI midyear estimate (**3,812,518**) remains a separate series and is not copied to local areas.

Eight Geostat thematic catalogue pages list **48 original XLSX tables**: geography/internal migration 6, demographic/social 7, education 3, economic 8, international migration 6, households 1, living conditions 13, disability 4. `GEO_GEOSTAT2024_CATALOG_INVENTORY.json` privately records each URL, title, receipt, SHA-256 and size. All 48 originals were acquired privately; `GEO_GEOSTAT2024_FIELD_INVENTORY.json` mechanically inventories their sheets, columns and **85,449 numeric cells**, including numeric header cells. That cell count is neither an observation count nor a semantic acceptance. Five selected tables were checked and partially adopted; **43 table bodies remain semantically unassessed**. Original XLSX files and the candidate dataset are excluded from Git. [Geostat's terms](https://www.geostat.ge/en/page/monacemta-gamoyenebis-pirobebi) permit statistical-data reuse with attribution while reserving third-party material and protected marks; redistribution and each original's conditions still require checking before hosting.

It adds **22 indicators and 1,740 observations**, of which **1,440 are source reported** and **300 are AreaData calculated**. That arithmetic checks a source-row hierarchy; it is **not** an official administrative code or boundary certification.

## Geographic and planning limits

The Geostat source tables identify names and row order but do not provide a verified administrative code or year-compatible polygon crosswalk. Tbilisi is both a self-governing municipality and one top-level statistical row; its ten districts are internal diagnostic units, not ten separate municipalities. Twelve 2015 geoBoundaries reference shapes were left **unjoined** because their edition, coverage and occupied-territory treatment differ. The [Geostat GIS portal](https://gis.geostat.ge/en/) and [API documentation](https://gis-api.geostat.ge/) are locations to investigate, not acquired 2024 polygons. The country site explicitly displays the boundary gap and keeps source-qualified IDs distinct.

The [current English consolidation of the Spatial Planning, Architectural and Construction Activities Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/4276845?publication=39) was located. Article 28 describes municipal preparation of spatial plans; Article 31 describes concept/final stages and Agency opinion before Sakrebulo approval. Article 35 sets ordinary municipal urban-planning competence, while Article 36 provides a special-regulation exception. [Government Ordinance 260](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4579368?publication=0) and the [Local Self-Government Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2244429) are also official locations. Their operative amendments, each municipality's applicable duty, current guidance, full plan bodies and decisions need legal and area-specific audit before adopting planning metadata. The [Tbilisi 2019 land-use plan record](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4508064), [Tbilisi 2026 initial budget record](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6712283) and [Batumi budget portal](https://batumi.gov.ge/budget) are location leads only. The Tbilisi budget's later consolidated amount is not verified; no amount, implementation, evaluation, approved selected-area plan or formal status was imported. Zero collected documents does not mean zero existing plans.

The next work is to semantically classify the other 43 tables, prioritize useful local sector fields, obtain official code and 2024-compatible geometry, verify planning authority and actual municipality plan/fiscal/performance contents, and complete all applicable 42 acceptance scenarios plus an independent `ACCEPT`. No hosting or public update is authorized by this partial check.
