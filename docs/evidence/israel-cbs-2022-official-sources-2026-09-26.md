## Geographic scope and reconciliation

The CBS broad table has a Nationwide row and six district rows (codes 1–6), plus a separately reported **Judea and Samaria Area**, code 7. The six district populations sum to **9,119,780**. The separate row is **481,940**, and together they match the source's **9,601,720** Nationwide value. The separately reported area remains source-only: it is not turned into a seventh district, assigned a provider polygon, or omitted from the national source scope. This is a description of the CBS table, not an inference about sovereignty or a legal boundary.

The six district names were matched to six `geoBoundaries` 2017 reference shapes for navigation. The CBS codes and source names are kept in `ISR_CBS_2022_DISTRICT_CROSSWALK.csv`. Dated legal-boundary equivalence, local-authority membership, territorial exceptions, and a usable polygon for the separate reporting area remain unverified. The [CBS geographic dictionary API documentation](https://www.cbs.gov.il/en/cbsNewBrand/Pages/API-Dictionary.aspx) identifies district, subdistrict, locality and planning-committee code/year resources; their current response bodies and territorial versions have not been acquired. National WDI population is a different series and period and is displayed as such.

## Planning, finance and local operations

The [Knesset's Planning and Building Law, 1965 record](https://main.knesset.gov.il/Activity/Legislation/Laws/pages/lawprimary.aspx?lawitemid=2000613&st=lawlaws&t=lawlaws) is an official law location. No specific plan, approval stage, target or implementation result was adopted. An audited financial report must not be labelled an approved budget, execution plan or official evaluation without reading its contents.

The legal planning unit may be a planning committee or local authority rather than a CBS district; that institutional mapping is open. The local site therefore has no verified local planning document. It shows an explicit missing-document state, not “no plans exist.” Budget, spending, implementation and evaluation remain unconfirmed.
