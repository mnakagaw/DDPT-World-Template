## Planning system and actual materials

The [Surabaya JDIH record](https://jdih.surabaya.go.id/others/3803) supplied an official hosted copy of Permendagri 86/2017: **644-page PDF, 3,719,199 bytes**, SHA-256 pinned in `config/indonesia-planning-source-manifest.json`. The cover and PDF pages 5–7, 15–16 were inspected. Page 5 defines BAPPEDA's coordination role; page 6 defines RPJPD as 20 years, RPJMD as five years and RKPD as one year; pages 15–16 discuss participatory/spatial approaches and provincial/kabupaten/kota planning. This is a national legal **reference**, displayed only at national scope. A ministry copy, current amendments, all clauses, forms and local application remain to be reviewed. It is not an approved local plan or evidence of execution.

The [Jawa Barat JDIH record](https://jdih.jabarprov.go.id/page/info/produk/53538?tentang=peraturan-daerah-provinsi-jawa-barat-nomor-7-tahun-2025-tentang-rencana-pembangunan-jangka-menengah-daerah-tahun-2025-2029) identifies Perda 7/2025 for the 2025–2029 RPJMD, with enactment date 25 September 2025 and an 809-page document; the [province's official plan archive](https://jabarprov.go.id/arsip-dan-dokumen?kategori=dokumen+perencanaan&tahun=2025) independently lists that item. A Bappeda 2026 amendment-consultation listing is a different, later stage and is not substituted for the 2025 Perda. No area-specific RKPD, APBD, expenditure, results or official evaluation is acquired.
