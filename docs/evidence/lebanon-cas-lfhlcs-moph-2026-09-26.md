## Workbook, fields and geographic meaning

The Demography XLS has 26 sheets (two table lists and HL1–HL24), 187 physically present columns and 136 columns with numeric cells under the workbook's layouts. `LBN_LFHLCS_DEMOGRAPHY_COLUMNS.csv` records every physical column, numeric/nonempty counts and a disposition. **Three** HL5 columns are adopted; all others are `unassessed`, including 23 other table sheets. Their presence is not semantic acceptance. The source HL5 cell values are weighted estimates in **thousands**. The importer multiplies by 1,000 and rounds to 100 people to match report Table 1.1 precision; it preserves the original cell value and locator for every observation.

HL5 has a national row, eight governorate total rows and 26 caza rows. Three columns × 35 areas yield **105** adopted survey observations. The national women, men and combined estimates are **2,499,400**, **2,343,100** and **4,842,500** people. Representative combined values are Beirut **341,700**, Mount Lebanon **2,032,600**, Akkar **324,000**, Keserwan **260,500** and Jbeil **129,500**. The script checks source unrounded governorate and district sums against parents before rounding. These 2018 survey estimates remain distinct from the World Bank's national 2025 population series. They cannot fill current-year governorate values or be renamed census counts.

The source reports **eight** governorates, while MOPH's undated service and the bootstrap `geoBoundaries` reference both show **nine**, including a separate Keserwan-Jbeil/KSH governorate. CAS's reporting governorates are source-native units with no asserted polygon. A 26-row CAS-to-MOPH caza name crosswalk and MOPH PCODEs are retained, but those codes are provider attributes, not certified CAS or legal 2018 codes. The 26 polygons are reference drawings. Each caza observation records the *survey* geographic edition separately from the polygon's *MOPH undated* edition. Consequently the original district value is visible with its source, while numeric polygon coloring and comparable within-governorate district ranking are withheld. A same-name geographic match does not establish legal or temporal equivalence.
