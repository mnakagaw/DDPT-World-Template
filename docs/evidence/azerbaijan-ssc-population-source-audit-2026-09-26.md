One missing sheet cannot establish the absence of other local statistics.

`scripts/import-azerbaijan-ssc-population.py` preserves **four local indicators and 403 direct observations**. All 2026 child values sum to their source parent values at the published 0.1-thousand precision; the reported 2019 national/parent rows remain direct values despite Aghdara's missing child. No incomplete child sum is used to populate a parent. The WDI national midyear series is in **people**, while SSC is in **thousand people** and has a different reference date; the candidate explicitly disables the generic cross-source population-difference card after a browser check found a false subtraction. No national WDI value is assigned to a local area.

The initial geoBoundaries `ADM1` result had just two 2021 components, contiguous Azerbaijan and Nakhchivan. They are combined into one **national location outline only**, not treated as administrative region shapes, 2026 legal boundaries or evidence for local code matching. Economic regions are SSC statistical groupings, not asserted legal plan makers. Local areas have no joined polygons. The [President's official Code page](https://president.az/az/articles/view/5773) locates spatial-planning Articles 17–20, and [ARXKOM's master-plan catalogue](https://arxkom.gov.az/en/sehersalma/bas-planlar) locates possible plan documents.
