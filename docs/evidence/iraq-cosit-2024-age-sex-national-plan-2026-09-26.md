## COSIT census age/sex and urban/rural table

- Official [COSIT census table catalogue](https://cosit.gov.iq/ar/1234-2019-09-05-07-53-20), direct [age/sex PDF](https://cosit.gov.iq/images/census2024/%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86_%D9%81%D8%A6%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf). Catalogue table index 21 in the captured manifest; 268 PDF pages. Acquired PDF SHA-256 `e0800bbfd58a41d879ed4b9edc269eefe37a632eb2d32ecbade2b3f171ea0b3f`.
- The adopted scope is the **18 printed governorate Total rows** and PDF page 268 Grand Total. The governorate total appears on PDF pages 18, 37, 74, 83, 111, 126, 140, 163, 175, 179, 191, 208, 216, 225, 233, 247, 256 and 268. PDF pages 18 and 268 were visually checked. The importer extracts those row labels/codes and pins the PDF hash and 268-page count. Full age-band and sub-governorate content is not yet inventoried.
- Nine printed numeric fields, in extracted left-to-right order: total; female; male; rural total, female, male; urban total, female, male. For every adopted row, female + male, rural + urban, and both crossed partitions reconcile to total. Each of the nine governorate-column sums equals the printed national row. All 18 governorate total populations uniquely match the separately acquired [2024 summary workbook](https://nogp.gov.iq/DatasetDetails.aspx?id=17). The printed two-digit COSIT census codes are recorded separately from the `geoBoundaries` 2022 provider polygon IDs.
- Adopted four **person counts**: male, female, urban and rural, each for country + 18 governorates (76 observations). Printed total is a cross-check of the pre-existing original census count, not a new indicator. The four sex-by-urban/rural counts were arithmetically checked but not adopted as indicators. National male 23,161,604; female 22,957,189; urban 32,363,649; rural 13,755,144; total 46,118,793. For Baghdad the respective values are 4,904,977; 4,875,452; 8,334,666; 1,445,763; total 9,780,429.
- The source's urban/rural classification is carried as printed; its detailed definition remains to be checked. It is not silently identified with WDI urbanization or an official boundary revision. The 18 codes and names match the 2024 census rows, but 2024 legal/census polygons and district units are not yet joined to the 2022 reference polygons.
- The 268-page table's age bands and smaller units, 23 other unacquired COSIT catalogue PDFs, and the acquired drinking-water and education tables remain outside adoption. The drinking-water PDF has primary and secondary source categories; its denominator/meaning has not yet been fully established and must not be equated with the distinct water-at-home measure or safe water. The 562-page final census report remains acquired but not table-inventoried. Source-specific redistribution terms remain unresolved.

The cover at PDF page 1 identifies the Republic of Iraq and Ministry of Planning; PDF page 2 states May 2024. PDF page 157 (printed page 156) has a spatial development programme with suggested Baghdad transport activities and named executing ministries. Pages 1, 2 and 157 were text-checked; page 157 was visually checked. The remaining pages/rows are not fully inventoried.
- The site adopts **one country-level document reference** with period 2024–2028, `availability: content_verified` for those pages and `official_status: unverified`. It does not attribute the national plan to Baghdad or infer local plan approval, budget allocation, actual execution or official evaluation from suggested national activities. Governorate plans, planning law/competent legal unit, budget, execution and evaluation records remain unverified.
