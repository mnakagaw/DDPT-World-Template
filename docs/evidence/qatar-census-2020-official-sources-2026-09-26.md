## Geography and planning

The candidate uses these eight as text/table selection areas, with official code and boundary edition **null** and zero adopted polygons. Its [zoning page](https://www.mm.gov.qa/QatarMasterPlan/English/msdp-zoning.aspx) describes Doha, Al Rayyan, Al Shehaniya, Al Daayen, Um Salal and Al Shamal, with Al Khor and Al Wakra described as future extensions; this page has not established current coverage/approval in 2026. The acquired [Doha Municipality Vision and Development Strategy PDF](https://www.mm.gov.qa/QatarMasterPlan/Downloads-qnmp/MunicipalityStrategy/English/Doha%20Dec%202017.pdf) is Volume 1 of a Doha spatial plan. **Its cover says 1 June 2014 although the URL filename says “Dec 2017”.** Cover and printed page 2 (PDF page 6) were visually checked; page 2 names strategic context, zones/regulations and zone maps and describes its intended effect. Current revision, legal status, geographic match to the 2020 census area, budgets, implementation and evaluation are unresolved. The PDF is therefore an acquired historical planning original and a lead, not a `dataset.documents` plan attached to the 2020 Doha statistic.
