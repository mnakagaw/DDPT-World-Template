# Sri Lanka DCS 2024 census source audit — 2026-09-27 JST

**Status: partial domestic candidate, not an independently accepted Sri Lanka edition; unpublished.** The separate candidate is `generated/sri-lanka-areadata-20260927` (ignored by Git). The original WDI national series remains distinct from the 2024 Census of Population and Housing (CPH) final counts.

## Catalogue, acquisition and field decisions

The [DCS CPH 2024 official catalogue](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Population/StaticalInformation/CPH2024/) was saved with a SHA-256 pinned HTML snapshot. `scripts/fetch-sri-lanka-cph2024.py` acquired **34 official originals**: seven Population A XLSX, 16 Housing A XLSX, nine GN XLSX, the [administrative-code XLSX](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/qlink/AdminDivCodes_Excel), and the [266-page English final report](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/Population/CPH_2024/CPH2024_Final_Eng.pdf). URL, bytes, SHA-256 and local ignored raw path are pinned in `config/sri-lanka-cph2024-source-manifest.json`; acquisition receipt is in the candidate's `evidence/LKA_CPH2024_ACQUISITION.json`. The source is publicly downloadable; raw redistribution terms have not been checked, so originals stay outside Git.

`scripts/inventory-sri-lanka-cph2024.py` records sheet names and declared extents for **33 XLSX**; a declared extent can include formatted blank cells. Only the following three numbered workbooks have a detailed numeric and semantic audit across all **366 reporting rows** each (country, 25 districts, 340 Divisional Secretary divisions):

These are census usual residents, not WDI midyear estimates. |

The [final report](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/Population/CPH_2024/CPH2024_Final_Eng.pdf), PDF page 68 (printed page 51), Table 3.2 directly supplies the nine province population values. This page was rendered and visually inspected. The other report tables and **20 numbered A workbooks** are acquired but `priority_unassessed`: only sheet extents, not all numerical cells and definitions, have been inventoried. The nine GN workbooks are also unadopted; the population and code sheets received a focused reconciliation described below, while other GN numeric fields await audit. Acquisition does not imply adoption or source-domain completion.

The selected 30 indicators produce **10,989 direct observations**: 30 × 366 country/district/DS values plus nine province population values. There are **375 statistical territories**: country, nine provinces, 25 districts, 340 DS divisions. Direct 2024 census population is **21,781,800**, including Western Province **6,117,341**, Colombo District **2,375,415** and Colombo DS **292,089**. National households are **6,111,315** in A14/A16. Province household and age values are **missing** because these three adopted originals do not directly report them at province level; no district sum is silently promoted to a province observation. Each original field, source cell, selected value and arithmetic check is retained in ignored `evidence/LKA_CPH2024_AUDIT.json`.

## Codes, geography and provisional GN series

The official code XLSX and [GN population workbook](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Population/StaticalInformation/CPH2024/GN_population_excel) each contain **14,008 GN code-component rows**, all joined by exact province/district/DS/GN components. One GN name differs (`Koratota`/`Korathota`). The 340 final A5 DS rows match code-register/ GN district, population and sex totals uniquely; 324 also match normalized DS names, and 16 have name variants retained in the audit. This supports a component-code crosswalk, **not** a claim that the code register and census use a legally identical boundary edition.

The GN population sheet labels itself **Provisional**. Its national population and DS sex totals agree with final A5, but age-group values differ in **eight DS divisions** (Colombo, Kaduwela, Maharagama, Mirigama, Minipe, Harispattuwa, Hambantota and Mahiyanganaya). These differences are recorded cell by cell in `LKA_CPH2024_AUDIT.json`. No GN observation is merged into the final A5 indicators. The initial provider ADM1 shapes were removed; no dated official polygon has been proven compatible with the adopted 2024 hierarchy. The site uses a no-boundary alternative. A DS statistical area is not automatically a Municipal Council, Urban Council or Pradeshiya Sabha; the GN-by-local-government workbook is acquired but unassessed.

## Planning and fiscal source boundary

The [official Gazette Extraordinary 2199/15](https://documents.gov.lk/view/extra-gazettes/2020/10/2199-15_E.pdf) is a **location-only** lead on municipal budget/development-plan rules. Its body, current force and applicability to a selected council have not been audited or adopted. Its dataset source `retrieved_at` marks the online location check, not PDF-body retrieval. [Colombo Municipal Council's administrative-services page](https://www.colombo.mc.gov.lk/administrative-services.php) identifies its planning/budget functions; the [2024 financial-statement PDF](https://www.colombo.mc.gov.lk/downloads/f24.pdf) and [2026 budget-inspection notice](https://documents.gov.lk/view/extra-gazettes/2025/11/2464-33_E.pdf) have official locations but their bodies were not added to this dataset. The notice is not the full budget, and a financial statement subject to audit is not an auditor's opinion. None is attached to Colombo District or Colombo DS. Applicable local-government laws, local plans, full budget bodies, actual expenditure and official evaluations need jurisdiction and period review.
