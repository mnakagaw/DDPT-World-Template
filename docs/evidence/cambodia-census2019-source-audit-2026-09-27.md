## Printed geography and value exceptions

The P tables use 2-digit province headers and 4/6-digit detail codes after zero-padding. The report overview states **204** district/city/khan units while the P-table roster has **202**; the two-unit discrepancy is unresolved and the P-table roster is not called a complete legal register. The current NCDD Gazetteer confirms **Srei Santhor district 0314** and communes `031401` onward, allowing the P-03 row printed as `211` to be identified from both the child prefixes and an independent official page. This one correction does not validate every current code or legal boundary.

The exact audit retains three `Toatl` labels (P-03, P-09, P-11), P-01's Banteay Meancheay title/body spelling difference, and P-19's **Preah Sihanouk title over Stung Treng body/code 19**. It reports **28 count-field parent/child discrepancies across eight parent IDs**: `03`, `0302`, `08`, `0801`, `14`, `17`, `22`, `2202`. District `0302` Chamkar Leu prints 5,160 in all four count fields, including male/female/population, and contradicts its children. District `0801` Kandal Stueng's printed counts are inconsistent with its children. District `2202` Banteay Ampil's population and sex fields conflict while its household count reconciles. The importer withholds all six fields at `0302` and `0801`, and the five population/sex/ratio/size fields at `2202`. All raw printed values and child sums remain in `KHM_CENSUS2019_AUDIT.json`; no child sum silently replaces a parent. Province direct values remain as published, with mismatch context shown in the comparison membership note.

The 2017 geoBoundaries ADM1 shapes produced by initial generation have no proven 2019 NIS code/edition match. They were removed. The **1,874 statistical reporting areas** (country + 25 + 202 + 1,646) therefore have **zero joined compatible official polygons**, and the site uses its no-boundary selector and complete member tables. District/municipality/khan and commune/sangkat legal subtypes are not assigned from names. The optional [NCDD Gazetteer index](https://db.ncdd.gov.kh/gazetteer/view/index.castle) and later [MEF 2025 code register](https://data.mef.gov.kh/datasets/pd_69d4f3d075bdc100087f426d) are source leads, not an automatic 2019 crosswalk.
