## Geography, codes and plan competence

The DOSM population files report 160 district rows in each selected year. Perlis and three Federal Territories are also whole-area state/FT rows; their district rows duplicate the same place and are not counted as a finer tier. Four 2020 district names differ from the 2024 roster, including Cameron Highlands/Cameron Highland and three Seberang Perai abbreviations. Older rows are held rather than name-joined. Each imported statistical district has a provider-row ID; it is **not** certified as a legal district/PBT code or planning authority.

[MyGeoportal's UPI register](https://www.mygeoportal.gov.my/en/unique-parcel-identifier-upi) was acquired as an XLSX (3,323 rows, 16 state-code and 139 land-district-code rows). Its land-administration version has no verified dated DOSM 2020/2024 statistical crosswalk. The 2017 geoBoundaries ADM1 reference shapes are withheld, including for apparent name matches. No polygon is silently assigned and official_code is null. Obtain DOSM/UPI/PBT codes, dated official boundary editions and Sabah/Sarawak changes before a map or legal unit claim.

[PLANMalaysia's local-plan overview](https://www.planmalaysia.gov.my/main/article/rancangan-tempatan) and [manual catalogue](https://www.planmalaysia.gov.my/main/documents?type=manual-perancangan) identify the Peninsular Act 172 framework, not a uniform law for Kuala Lumpur, Sabah, Sarawak or all Federal Territories. Old direct Act 172 and manual PDF URLs returned 404 HTML; neither text is marked acquired. The applicable local authority, current manual, plan period and budget competence require jurisdiction-specific inspection.

For Kuala Lumpur alone, the [Federal Gazette P.U. (B) 206/2025](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/files/senarai-perundangan/subsidiari/(10)-pemberitahuan-penerimaan-draf-pelan-tempatan-bagi-wpkl.pdf), English page 4, says the 2040 Local Plan was adopted on 5 May 2025 and took effect on 11 June 2025 under Act 267. [DBKL Local Plan 2040 Volume 2](https://ppkl.dbkl.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/1.-VOLUME-2-PROMOTING-CITY-DEVELOPMENT.pdf) was acquired (576 pages, cover/title verified); its tables, targets and implementation are **priority_unassessed**. [DBKL's 2025 budget speech](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/files/arkib-ucapan-belanjawan-dbkl/ucapan-belanjawan-2025.pdf), PDF page 16 paragraph 54, reports estimated revenue RM2.45 billion, estimated expenditure RM2.77 billion and a reported deficit RM325.9 million. The rounded billion figures do not exactly reproduce the published deficit; no recomputation or actual-spending claim is made. The [2014–2023 budget summary XLSX](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/files/data-terbuka/ringkasan-bajet-hasil-dan-belanja-dbkl.xlsx) has 40 numeric estimate cells, held as historical budget estimates. The [2023 DBKL annual report](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/files/laporan-tahunan-dbkl-2023_final.pdf) was acquired (467 pages); the financial statement section is scanned and actual expenditure or official evaluation was not extracted. Other states, PBTs and districts have no plan/finance material attributed by this adapter. No unacquired plan is called nonexistent or unapproved.
