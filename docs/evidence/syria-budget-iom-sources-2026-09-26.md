# Syria source audit: 2026 national budget and IOM humanitarian baseline

Checked 2026-09-26 for the ignored local candidate `generated/syria-areadata-20260926`. The candidate started with WDI **national** series and 14 `geoBoundaries` 2017 reference governorate shapes. It remains a source-research bootstrap, not an accepted domestic dashboard. Originals, receipts, rendered review pages and output hashes are outside Git in the candidate's `raw/` and `evidence/` directories.

## Syrian Ministry of Finance citizen budget

The [Ministry of Finance Citizen Budget 2026](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/citizen_budget_2026.pdf) returned a 52-page PDF (1,023,310 bytes; SHA-256 `9d460f4694527f2d8245a0869f9d3ef20b09e14eb806ddc4df4895d94f7409fd`). PDF pages 1, 17, 18 and 50 were visually checked. The cover names the Ministry and the 2026 citizen budget; page 17 presents national economic assumptions. Page 50 describes preparation of **14 governorate local development plans as a 2026 target** for the Planning and Statistics Authority. It does not show that those plans have been completed, approved or funded. The publication's national budget tables are not a governorate budget or expenditure series.

AreaData registers one **country-level budget reference** with period 2026, `availability: content_verified` for the checked passages and `official_status: unverified`. Its summary explicitly distinguishes the 14-plan target from completed plans. No governorate document, budget value or project execution figure is adopted. Legal planning competence, plan guidance, each actual local plan, budget, implementation and evaluation remain to be verified. Redistribution terms for the PDF also remain under review.

## IOM DTM round 17

The [IOM DTM June 2026 Syria report](https://dtm.iom.int/reports/syrian-arab-republic-population-mobility-and-baseline-assessment-round-17-01-30-june-2026) links to a [nine-page PDF](https://dtm.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1461/files/reports/DTM%20Syria_Baseline_Round17.pdf) (3,624,048 bytes; SHA-256 `0243b2e01aad3a91184d697090ab060ea50f7ce4ae8619eb49f3a18a38021474`). Page 7 was rendered and checked against extracted text: it includes a national row and 14 governorate rows with a population field and four rounded mobility shares. Page 8 describes a key-informant community assessment and its uncertainty. These humanitarian estimates are distinct from a census or the WDI national series. The report does not supply an official-code or 2026-to-2017 boundary crosswalk.

The [IOM DTM terms](https://dtm.iom.int/terms-conditions-use-and-privacy-policy-dtm-website-and-information-products) were acquired and checked (HTML SHA-256 `d062f8f595ac0342e300ff1dea3e364c4ca375744c37e84d0a38f7e900c49717`). They allow non-commercial viewing/downloading but require **prior written permission for extraction and redistribution**. Consequently, the report is a source lead only: **zero IOM numeric observations or indicators were adopted or published**. The private audit identifies the report's numeric field groups and records the reason for nonadoption; it does not transfer the table to Git or Kit. Future reuse must resolve the terms and geographic correspondence first.

## Statistical portal and candidate verification

An [official SANA announcement](https://sana.sy/locals/2478248/) names the Planning and Statistics Authority's new portal and says it hosts statistical books, surveys, census and plans. The announced `https://psc.gov.sy/site2/arabic` path returned HTTP 404 on this check. That failed retrieval does not establish absence of the underlying official publications. The old UNSD-listed `cbssyr.org` address was not treated as a verified current government catalogue. No census table, current official geographic code register or local statistical value was acquired.

The local dataset has 15 territories, 12 indicators, 312 WDI observation records and one national document. Country validation returned zero errors and one expected national-only warning; the site built. In a local browser, selecting Aleppo showed zero local indicators and no Aleppo document, while the national budget appeared separately under **National reference materials**. The national budget planning HTML/documents CSV includes the correct source and 2026 period; the Aleppo outputs omit the national document. The candidate dataset SHA-256 is `a5e30192a35c4817284c1fed6a17514d3b2c72094d865ade646d68815470c9da`. These are scoped checks, not independent acceptance or public verification.

Next: obtain current authority census/sector originals and official administrative codes, investigate each local plan and budget publication by entity and period, and repeat output and independent acceptance checks. The Kit feedback carries only the public Ministry/IOM URLs and restrictions at `official_location_identified`.
