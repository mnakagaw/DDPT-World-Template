## Planning and finance follow-up

The ignored `evidence/CYP_PLANNING_FOLLOWUP_2026-09-27.md` and `raw/planning-followup/receipt.json` retain 16 URL attempts: nine originals with SHA-256, seven HTTP 403 or timeout. [Municipalities Law 52(I)/2022, art. 92](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2022_1_52/full.html) requires each municipality to prepare a medium-term strategic plan with objectives/indicators and an annual budget; art. 93 makes municipal-district components part of a unified municipality budget. [Communities Law 86(I)/1999, art. 64](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1999_1_86/full.html) creates a separate community annual-budget route. The statutes do not prove a particular published plan or one universal plan cycle.

The [Pafos 2026 budget entry](https://pafos.org.cy/proipologismoi-dimou-pafou/) led to a 64-page budget PDF and a three-page, **conditional** 2026-02-27 ministerial approval letter. The budget's page 1 and page 2 surplus values disagree by €30,000/€20,000/€10,000 for 2026/2027/2028. Neither source resolves that contradiction, so no amount or individual project approval was adopted. [Larnaka 2025 council minutes](https://www.larnaka.org.cy/wp-content/uploads/10.pdf) confirm strategic-plan approval, and [Limassol 2025](https://www.limassol.org.cy/uploads/Praktika_DS/6d28346e34.pdf)/[2026 minutes](https://www.limassol.org.cy/uploads/Praktika_DS/6fe96c0d03.pdf) confirm strategy/objectives decisions; the actual plan annexes were not acquired. No municipality-level audited actuals, implementation result or official evaluation was acquired. These current municipality materials are **not** attached to historic Census locality IDs.

The direct [Pafos budget PDF](https://pafos.org.cy/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Budget-2026-Template-v7.-09.10.2025-v.5-%CE%93%CE%99%CE%91-%CE%94%CE%97%CE%9C%CE%9F%CE%A3%CE%99%CE%95%CE%A5%CE%A3%CE%97_compressed.pdf) and [approval letter PDF](https://pafos.org.cy/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/27022026-%CE%88%CE%B3%CE%BA%CF%81%CE%B9%CF%83%CE%B7-%CE%A0%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%8B%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%BB%CE%BF%CE%B3%CE%B9%CF%83%CE%BC%CE%BF%CF%8D-%CE%B3%CE%B9%CE%B1-%CF%84%CE%BF-%CE%AD%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%82-2026-%CE%BA%CE%B1%CE%B9-%CE%9C%CE%94%CE%A0-2026-2028_compressed.pdf) are the acquired originals.
