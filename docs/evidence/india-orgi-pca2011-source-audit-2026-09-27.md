## Planning law, guidance and actual materials

The official [Constitution of India PDF](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/359f70a69695affb9d72f8393102bd2e.pdf), 799 pages, SHA-256 `485d637c268bd079cf07a81e7a1df05ea785e3effda167a4f19699e97279ee50`, was acquired. Article **243G**, printed p. 122/PDF p. 301, allows state law to endow Panchayats with preparation of economic/social plans; Article **243W**, printed p. 130/PDF p. 317, addresses Municipalities and state legislative devolution. This is a national constitutional framework, not proof of the operative plan duty, boundary, or approved plan in each state, Panchayat or municipality. Applicable state and urban laws remain unacquired.

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj [FY 2026–27 Panchayat Development Plan booklet](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/document/preparation-of-panchayat-development-plan-booklet-2026-27/) was acquired as an 88-page PDF, SHA-256 `1fead200a14afc2c68b8aeddf3548f1f03eab4b36dbf44e105016309cc4bcb61`. Chapters 3–4 distinguish Gram Panchayat Development Plan from Block and District Panchayat Development Plans; chapter 6 covers uploading/validation. The [eGramSwaraj 2026–27 prerequisite note](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/document/panchayat-planning-for-fy-2026-27-prerequisites-and-key-updates-in-egramswaraj-portal/) was acquired as a three-page PDF, SHA-256 `dce9dd23a817e60b0f360f280155058a0f6940c54b24050ca42141be7c1895db`, covering Panchayat profile, resource envelope, budget allocation and portal changes. These are national rural planning references. [eGramSwaraj](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/e-gramswaraj/) is an identified plan/progress/accounting portal; no area-specific approved plan, budget, expenditure or evaluation body was acquired. No 2011 census district was silently treated as a present District Panchayat.

The dataset's planning page offers the national materials in a separately labelled reference section and an evidence/HTML fallback for the selected historical area. It reports zero of 675 local areas with verified material references. It does not certify any area plan, fiscal status, completion or approval. Source-specific original redistribution terms need review before publishing source bodies; the code/data candidate is unpublished.
