The FCSC report labels the 2005 values **census data (December)**. SCAD labels 2024 as integrated administrative registers and 2023 as revised R1 results. The World Bank 2005 national midyear estimate in the bootstrap is **4,664,790**, separate from FCSC's 4,106,427 census count. The difference is not treated as a correction, increase, or local allocation. `scripts/import-uae-historical-and-scad-population.py` parses the pinned originals and retains PDF page/row and SCAD table locators; the private `evidence/ARE_SOURCE_TABLE_AUDIT.json` keeps all four inspected FCSC year columns and the SCAD table fields.

The seven emirate shapes are **2017 geoBoundaries reference polygons** matched by name only. They are not official 2005/current legal boundaries. The three SCAD regions are source-scoped IDs with no joined polygons or confirmed official geographic codes. A statistical region does not automatically become a legal planning body. The [UAE government's local-government overview](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/the-uae-government/the-local-governments-of-the-seven-emirates) describes emirate-specific institutions; the [Dubai 2040 plan overview](https://u.ae/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/transport-and-infrastructure/dubai-2040-urban-master-plan) is a source lead, not an adopted local plan in this candidate. Relevant law, actual plan text, budget, implementation and official evaluation still need collection for each selected emirate.

The [FCSC UAE.Stat explorer](https://uaestat.fcsc.gov.ae/en) advertises national population-estimate series through 2024. Its subnational availability and definitions were **not verified** here. Do not use its national number as a local value. Current, comparable all-emirate statistics and local government outputs remain required before acceptance.
