The module enumerates **91 entries per province: two report links and 89 XLSX tables**. Seven provinces use the same table catalogue. The [NSO geographical-code publication](https://nsonepal.gov.np/content/7694/7694-geographical-codes-map-provi/) links an official 2023 XLS with seven `pradesh_code`, 77 `district_code` and 753 `localunit_code` records. Acquisition URL, UTC time, HTTP status, media type, byte length and SHA-256 of all 28 selected XLSX originals are in `generated/nepal-areadata-20260927/evidence/NPL_NSO2021_ACQUISITION.json`; the reproducible pins are `config/nepal-nso2021-source-manifest.json`. Explicit redistribution terms for the original workbooks are unverified; raw originals are ignored by Git.

## Population universe and geography

NSO's direct 2021 national population is **29,164,578**, with **6,666,937 all households**. The separate institutional rows contain **239,098 persons and 6,096 households**. Hhld06/Hhld09 cover **6,660,841 private households**; the 6,096-household difference is exactly the institutional-household count. District population includes local and institutional rows, while drinking-water/toilet district counts are over private households alone. Every one of the selected fields reconciles through its proper source hierarchy. WDI midyear population, NSO 2021 enumeration and current resident population must not be silently equated.

The 2021 workbook row ordinal and 2023 NSO code were matched by province/district hierarchy and local name. All **77 district names match** after normalization. Of 753 local rows, **749 match exactly after normalization; two differ only in minor transliteration** (`11405` Limchunbung/Limchungbung; `60702` Kuse/Kushe). The remaining two are **unresolved name conflicts**: 2021 `Kalika Gaunpalika` versus 2023 `Shuva Kalika Rural Municipality` for candidate `30304`, and 2021 `Dodhara Chandani Municipality` versus 2023 `Mahakali Urban Municipality` for candidate `70905`. These candidate numbers are retained for audit but `official_code` is null in the adopted 2021 territory records until historical identity is checked. The 2023 province code sheet calls them State 1–7, while the census tables label them Koshi through Sudurpaschim; ordinal correspondence is retained with that caveat.

The initial site's 2020 geoBoundaries ADM1 provider polygons were **removed**. No official polygon with proved compatibility to the 2021 census and code register has been adopted. The site displays a no-boundary alternative with fully functional hierarchy selectors and text/CSV output. Statistical districts and institutional rows are not automatically legal planning bodies or mapped municipalities.
