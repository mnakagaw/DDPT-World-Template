# Jordan: DoS 2025 population estimates and official source audit

Checked 2026-09-26. This is a **partial country candidate**, not a completed or published Jordan edition. The raw PDF and receipt are kept only in the ignored local `generated/jordan-areadata-20260926/raw/` tree. No large or unreviewed original is transferred to Git or Kit.

## Acquired source and adopted fields

- [Jordan Department of Statistics, estimated population at end of 2025](https://dosweb.dos.gov.jo/DataBank/population/population_Estimares/PopulationEstimates.pdf): January 2026, seven PDF pages, 144,275 bytes, SHA-256 `659587a2f4a243c912835720073c76974dd64bdb44dbab0411238cf425deacce`. Direct official PDF and receipt are local. Page 3 was rendered and inspected visually; page 7 was also rendered to confirm the age and area table headings. The importer reads page 3 from the hash-checked original.
- Table 2.2 gives country and 12 governorate end-2025 **estimates** of total, female and male population. Table 2.3 gives total, rural and urban counts. Its footnote defines urban as localities of at least 5,000 persons under the 2015 census definition. Five distinct count indicators were adopted, with 13 observations each: **65 domestic observations**.
- Printed national totals: total **11,937,000**, female **5,617,100**, male **6,319,900**, rural **1,152,300**, urban **10,784,700**. For each of the 12 governorates, female + male = total and rural + urban = total. Each governorate column sums to the corresponding printed national total. Table 2.3 total and Table 2.6 population repeat the same count and were not imported again.
- DoS's [2015 census catalogue](https://dosweb.dos.gov.jo/censuses/population_housing/census2015/census2015_tables/) is a separate survey and reference date. No 2015 census numeric table was acquired or adopted in this pass. The 2025 PDF's Table 2.1 itself distinguishes the 2015 census result from the separate 2015 end-year estimate. WDI midyear national values also remain separate; the app must not merge them into these end-year DoS counts.

The full table and numeric-field disposition is in the ignored local `generated/jordan-areadata-20260926/evidence/INDICATOR_INVENTORY.csv`. It covers all tables in the PDF: Table 2.1 selected-year national sex series (not adopted pending method/period review); Tables 2.2 and 2.3 (five count columns adopted, rounded share not adopted); Table 2.4 administrative divisions (not adopted pending official lower-unit code and hierarchy); Table 2.5 national age/sex counts, percentages and sex ratio (not adopted as local values); Table 2.6 repeated population, area and density (population not duplicated; area/density not adopted pending boundary/denominator review). This inventory does not claim that the much larger 2015 census catalogue has been fully processed.

## Geography and planning

- The [Ministry of Interior's governorate list](https://moi.gov.jo/En/List/Governorates_and_Sectors) confirms 12 named governorates. The DoS names Jarash, Ajlun and Tafiela correspond to the 2006 `geoBoundaries` reference labels Jerash, Ajloun and Tafilah. All 12 names are in the local crosswalk, but neither the DoS table nor the provider polygon gives a verified official code and 2025 legal boundary equivalence. The polygons are navigation references only; do not use them for certified area or density.
- The [Ministry of Local Administration legislation page](https://www.mola.gov.jo/Ar/Pages/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86) lists Local Administration Law No. 22 of 2021. The ministry also exposes a [2018 governorate strategic and implementation plan guide](https://www.mola.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/guide_for_the_preparation_of_governorate_strategic_development_and_implementation_plans.pdf), which refers to the 2015 decentralization law; its current applicability needs review. The [Planning and International Cooperation Law No. 10 of 2024](https://www.mop.gov.jo/En/Pages/Planning_Law) is a national institutional source, not evidence of an individual governorate plan.
- The Prime Ministry [Official Gazette index](https://www.pm.gov.jo/Ar/Pages/NewsPaperDetails/6072) lists Local Administration Law No. 16 of 2026. The [Jordan News Agency's publication notice](https://org.petra.gov.jo/Include/InnerPage.jsp?ID=89470&lang=en&name=en_news) says it takes effect 60 days after publication on 2026-09-10, so it was **not yet effective on 2026-09-26**. The full law, transitional provisions and implications for each planning unit remain to be reviewed. No selected governorate or municipal plan, budget, expenditure, implementation report or official evaluation was acquired. The site shows a working fallback rather than a claim that a plan does not exist.

## Local verification and open gates

The Jordan candidate has 13 territories, 17 indicators (12 WDI national series plus five DoS end-year estimates), 377 observation records (312 WDI records including explicit missing entries plus 65 DoS counts), 21 registered sources and zero plan documents. Dataset SHA-256 after integration: `bcd53b8268c7f5570399230a0cd59355bf9e3892259aeaa0e15411fdae93ad72`. `validate-country` returned zero errors and one expected missing-planning warning. Site build, `npm run check`, and 218/218 tests passed. The output verifier checked Jordan, Amman, Ajloun and Tafilah: each selected-area CSV value/year/source, the country's full 12-row comparison per DoS indicator, and HTML/evidence output identity. National diagnostic CSV had 221 data rows; the three selected governorate CSVs each had 17. A local browser showed Jordan's 11,937,000 DoS estimate alongside the separate 11,520,684 WDI midyear value, switched Jordan → Amman (5,004,600) → Jordan, and showed no acquired Amman plan or false approval state.
