## Field, geography and arithmetic controls

Table 02 columns B–J are total, male, female, urban total/male/female, rural total/male/female. Table 06 B–J are all IDPs, previously Abkhazia, previously Tskhinvali; the same three fields for male and female. The column register identifies every acquired numeric column, including all unadopted age and origin columns. For every populated Table 02 row, sex and urban/rural identities were checked with the source's dash meaning respected; Table 06 sex/origin identities were checked. Printed regional sums and their child sums match. The national Table 02 total is **3,929,581**, urban **2,455,444**, rural **1,474,137**. Table 06 national IDPs are **210,628**, a subset of usual residents, never added to population.

Table 02 reports nation + 11 disjoint regional/self-governing-city units + 63 other city/municipal units + 10 Tbilisi internal districts = **85 territory rows**. The 11 broad rows mix Tbilisi city, Adjara Autonomous Republic and nine statistical regions; the analysis contract permits their same-definition census comparison while preserving different legal roles. The 10 Tbilisi district population rows form a separate municipal-district level. Table 06 has no district detail, so its values remain `not_collected` at that level. Table 01 crosschecks all 85 Table 02 area totals; `Adjara A.R.`/`Autonomous Republic of Adjara`, `Imereti`/`ImereTi`, and `Sighnagi`/`Sighnaghi Municipality` are three explicitly reviewed label variants, not official-code matches.

The census excludes occupied territories. Source labels and printed parent order are retained as **source-scoped IDs**, with no claim that they are official administrative codes. [NSDI/Georgian public registry portal](https://nsdi.gov.ge/en/geoportal) identifies official municipality and region layer locations, but dated geometry, code fields, inclusion rules and a 2024 census match were not acquired. Twelve bootstrap 2015 provider polygons are withheld; the candidate adopts **zero polygons**. [Geostat annual estimates](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/41/population) and WDI estimates have different reference moments and remain separate from the 14 November 2024 census. The [2024 census catalogue](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/12/2024-population-census-of-georgia-results), [agricultural results catalogue](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/905/2024-agricultural-census-results), and [regional portal](https://regions.geostat.ge/regions/) need further table-level audit; the six workbooks do not exhaust those publications.
