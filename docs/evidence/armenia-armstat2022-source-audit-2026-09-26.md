Mechanical inventory is not semantic assessment: only selected 2022 cells of two Chapter 1 workbooks are adopted; 2001/2011 columns, other rows, and the remaining workbooks require field-by-field review. ## Geography and codes

The acquired [ARLIS 2025 incorporation of HD 002-2021 administrative classifier](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/208945), SHA-256 `ef45a3e7741ec5948e2bcab4616947065448f20e146b6789b7a3f470fbc162b9`, supplies ten marz codes, Yerevan's community code, 12 Yerevan district codes and 70 marz-community codes. Census rows and classifier entries were checked by parent marz, complete within-marz ordering, and English/Armenian names. The ignored `evidence/ARM_ARMSTAT2022_CODE_CROSSWALK.json` stores all 70 code matches and workbook hashes. The census Chapter 1 narrative says **72 communities**, while its full population table and the 2025 classifier yield **70 marz communities plus Yerevan = 71**. The differing statement and possible later territorial changes remain unresolved; the 2025 code join does not certify exact 2022 boundary equivalence.

No source-versioned official marz/community/district polygons were acquired. The generator's 2005 geoBoundaries ADM1 shapes were deliberately left unjoined, and the local site explicitly states that no verified map boundary is available. National, marz, community and Yerevan district are distinct geography types. The city community is a planning unit; districts are internal diagnostic units. A code match does not make a polygon or statutory boundary match.
