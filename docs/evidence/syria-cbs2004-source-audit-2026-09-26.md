# Syria historical census source audit — 2026-09-26

Scope: producer-side audit for an **unpublished partial** AreaData candidate. The 2004 population and housing census is historical. Its local counts do not estimate the population after conflict, displacement or changed administrative conditions. The project's first `SOURCE_PREFLIGHT.md`/JSON had no pre-researched Syria-specific lead. The former CBS domain did not serve the original PDFs in this session (HTTP 403 for `cbssyr.sy`); preserved copies of the *original CBS PDF pages* were acquired from the Internet Archive. The current `cbssyr.org` homepage is unrelated to the old statistical site, so it was not used as a current official endpoint.

The six XLS sheets are `Pop by Gov` (2010 civil registration, 7 columns), `Palestinian Refugees` (2009, 7), `Admin1_Governorate` (14 data rows, 170 columns), `Admin2_District` (61, 173), `Admin3_Sub-district` (270, 176) and `Admin4_City` (6,135, 13). The first two are different periods/populations. The City/locality identity and coverage are unverified, so none of its numeric fields is shown. All 167 numeric columns in each of the Admin1–3 sheets are recorded individually in the field ledger, with numeric-cell counts and an adoption decision. That is a **mechanical inventory**, not semantic approval of the remaining fields or of the whole census catalogue. Other CBS thematic tables and current statistical releases remain to inspect.

## Numeric reconciliation and adopted meaning

- The governorate PDF's national total is **17,920,844 people**, **9,196,878 male**, **8,723,966 female**, **3,150,358 households**, **3,006,885 occupied** and **372,003 vacant dwellings** in 2004. Its 14 governorate rows sum to every national source column. Population is persons enumerated in the historical census, not today's residents.
- The PDF and OCHA mirror differ in **213 field-level values** across 73 source rows: three housing fields in two governorates, three population/sex fields in 24 districts and three in 46 sub-districts. The private `evidence/SYR_CBS2004_AUDIT.json` keeps each pair. For example, the CBS PDF has Homs district **1,035,055** and Kherbet Tin Noor **52,496** people; the mirror has 1,035,438 and 52,879. The PDF values control the candidate; no silent repair was made.
- The row crosswalk finds 199 exact normalized Arabic name matches, 69 extraction/spelling variants and two visually reviewed exceptions. Full national → governorate → district → sub-district population and sex sums were checked. The six adopted indicators yield **1,083** observations: six at national plus 14 governorates, three at 61 districts and 270 sub-districts. The other XLS numeric fields remain inventoried and unadopted.
- The 14 governorate polygons come from a **2017 geoBoundaries reference** and were name-matched to 2004 CBS rows. They are navigation/display shapes, not certified 2004 or current legal boundaries. Districts and sub-districts have no joined polygon. The historical CBS/OCHA identifiers do not establish the current official territorial code system.
- World Bank national population time series and the 2004 CBS census are separate observations with different definitions and periods. They must not be merged into a 2004–2025 change rate or projected to local areas.

## Planning and further source leads

The [Syrian official news agency's October 2025 report](https://sana.sy/locals/2313737/) describes discussion of a *proposed* method for governorate local-development plans. It is a lead for the current planning apparatus, not the method's adopted text, an enacted planning law, an approved plan, a local budget or an evaluation. The current Planning and Statistics Authority and relevant local administration responsibilities require direct instrument and document review. No selected-area planning document was content-verified. The [Ministry of Finance 2026 citizen budget](https://docs.mof.gov.sy/citizen_budget_2026.pdf) is a national document location, not evidence of a chosen governorate's budget or execution; its table fields remain unassessed.

The ignored country project retains three originals, acquisition receipts, normalized rows, 213-line discrepancy report, a generated site and representative export files. `scripts/fetch-syria-census-2004.py` enforces the pinned hashes and refuses changed bodies; `scripts/audit-syria-census-2004.py` regenerates this inventory and the private row audit; `scripts/import-syria-census-2004.py` adopts only the stated fields. Redistribution terms for the originals still require review, so they are not in Git.
