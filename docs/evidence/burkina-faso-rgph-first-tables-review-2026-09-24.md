# Burkina Faso RGPH 2019: printed-page review of Tables I.1-I.7

Review date: 2026-09-24. Source: INSD, *Résultats du 5e RGPH: Volume des tableaux statistiques*, local acquired PDF `generated/burkina-faso-20260924/raw/insd-rgph-2019/statistical_tables.pdf`, SHA-256 `a665f61e49581cb39e85fdd3d70cb699acf3ec7b964dfbb2852fd35cfc19d7f2`. Physical PDF pages 27-29 were rendered and inspected; the printed footers identify them as report pages 3-5. `TABLE_COLUMN_SCREEN.csv` now records the physical first PDF page for every one of the 581 table headings. This review covers **seven** tables only; it does not certify the other tables or adopt a new indicator.

| Table, PDF page | Printed geographic scope, period and numeric columns | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I.1, 27 | Burkina Faso, 2019; ordinary-household residence status; male count/share, female count/share, combined count/share. The `Population de droit (RP+RA)` row is 20,505,155; the broader enumerated row including visitors is 20,628,164. | Cross-check only. The adopted 2019 resident population corresponds to `Population de droit`, not the visitor-inclusive count. Residence-status percentages have different denominators from a regional population share and are not imported under the existing population indicator. |
| I.2, 27 | Burkina Faso, 2019; residents of **collective households** by type and urban/rural; sex percentages and counts. The rows include refugee-camp (1,062) and displaced-camp (21,515) residents within this housing classification. | Deferred. These are residents of enumerated collective-household camp categories, **not** all refugees or all internally displaced persons. No UNHCR/IDP indicator is inferred from them. Category coverage and denominator require an explicit new definition before use. |
| I.3, 28 | Burkina Faso and urban/rural by sex; 2006 and 2019 counts, average annual growth for 2006-2019 and 1996-2006. | Deferred as a historical series until census methods and geographic comparability are checked; no 2006 value is silently given a 2019 administrative identity. |
| I.4, 28 | Burkina Faso, 2019; urban/rural and selected cities; male/female/total counts and sex shares, shares of national and urban population. National male 9,900,847 + female 10,604,308 = 20,505,155. | Cross-check only. The adopted sex totals match; urban/rural and named-city shares have their own populations and are not relabelled as administrative-region observations. |
| I.5, 28 | Thirteen 2019 historical regions plus Burkina Faso; resident population count and region's percentage of the 20,505,155 national total. | Cross-check only. All 13 printed counts match adopted `BFA_RGPH2019_POP_TOTAL` observations. The rounded percentage column is not a separate adopted indicator. |
| I.6, heading 28 / rows 29 | Burkina Faso and 13 historical regions, 2019; urban male, female and total counts; rural male, female and total counts. | Reviewed candidate, **not adopted**. All 14 urban + rural totals equal I.5; male and female components reconcile with I.7 and adopted observations. Before adding an urbanisation indicator, confirm the census definition of urban/rural and how it applies to 2019 local geography. |
| I.7, 29 | Continuation of I.6: Burkina Faso and 13 historical regions, 2019; combined male, female and total counts. | Cross-check only. Every row equals the adopted 2019 population and sex counts. This continuation is not an additional independent sample. |

The repeatable check is `scripts/country-burkina-faso/verify-first-census-tables.py`, copied into the ignored country project's `scripts/`. It parses all 14 named rows from I.5, I.6 and I.7, checks six component identities and compares them with adopted total/male/female 2019 observations. Its ignored output `evidence/FIRST_CENSUS_TABLES_CROSSCHECK.csv` contains the source numbers, territory IDs, equality result, and explicit `new_urban_rural_indicator_adopted=no`. It passed for all 14 rows. The script does not replace the visual check of the printed headings and geography.

For the 566 tables previously pending numeric-column review, this review resolves I.1-I.7 to a documented decision or deferred candidate. At this stage **559 other tables remained without printed-page review**; the [subsequent priority review](burkina-faso-rgph-priority-tables-review-2026-09-24.md) reduced the current number to 556. I.2, I.3 and I.6 still require definition/comparability work before any adoption; the seven table decisions are not seven new indicators. A table already represented by another acquired source remains in the inventory with its cross-check decision rather than being deleted.
