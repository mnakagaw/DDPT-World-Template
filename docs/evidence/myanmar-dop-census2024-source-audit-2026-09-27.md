# Myanmar: DOP 2024 census source and adoption audit (2026-09-27)

**Decision:** unpublished AreaData partial candidate. The 2024 census Union Report and demographic appendix support a Union plus 15 state/region-equivalent reporting-roster comparison. The combined population is **enumerated plus statistically estimated**, with very unequal field coverage. No official code/polygon join, local planning law/approved plan, or independent `ACCEPT` is claimed.

## Pinned originals and edition boundary

The [DOP demographic Excel appendix](https://dop.gov.mm/sites/dop.gov.mm/files/datamap-documents/2024_census_appendix_table_demographic_characteristics_eng.xlsx) was downloaded through Windows TLS verification from its [official Excel catalogue](https://www.dop.gov.mm/en/data-and-maps-category/2024-main-report-excel): 197,800 bytes, SHA-256 `b6e58696d96a706944925f49379dd52b565ff591c2cf083391a803a282c6e909`. The [DOP Union Report, 26 November file](https://www.dop.gov.mm/sites/dop.gov.mm/files/publication_docs/2024mphc_unionreport_en_26nov.pdf), linked by its [publication page](https://www.dop.gov.mm/en/publication/2024-myanmar-population-and-housing-census-union-report), is 8,349,430 bytes, SHA-256 `afa0ede888e9db51c262ee2affcc963d5f325ba86be9e0014b31cd4d9e546168`. The [earlier provisional report](https://dop.gov.mm/sites/dop.gov.mm/files/publication_docs/2024_provisional_result_eng.pdf) is 6,067,644 bytes, SHA-256 `571da504753ed2a1b5a3d329db39f5c0610a62ff100a4d770ccb4e794eebcf3a`. Their exact paths, dates, URLs, byte counts and hashes are in `config/myanmar-census2024-source-manifest.json`; the originals and audit JSON are kept in the ignored country candidate.

The provisional report prints **51,316,756**, while the later Union Report Table 2.2 and demographic appendix Table A-1 print **51,375,327**: a **58,571** difference between editions. Only the later report/appendix pair is adopted. The provisional edition remains evidence of the version distinction, not a second observation in the same series. The Union Report identifies midnight **30 September 2024** as the census reference time. It reports **152/330 townships fully enumerated**, **120 partially enumerated**, **58 not canvassed**. The Union total consists of **32,183,599 enumerated people (62.6%)** and **19,191,728 statistically estimated people (37.4%)**. The estimate uses remote sensing, GIS and demographic methods for areas not canvassed (sections 1.2.9 and 2.8). The report's own three-tier interpretation places Chin, Kayah, Sagaing, Rakhine and other low-coverage areas below 55% field enumeration; its local totals require particular caution.

The [Ministry of Information FY2026-27 national/local planning coordination report](https://www.moi.gov.mm/moi:eng/news/18967) was also acquired as HTML: 27,993 bytes, SHA-256 `cc9893574a97acd303ca184d9dcc7dbd341f3a62b0832ca47e1a9e3b56cde5e8`. It mentions Union ministries and Region/State governments presenting planning work. It is neither a planning law, an approved regional plan, a budget nor a performance evaluation.

## Whole-workbook inventory and adopted cells

`scripts/audit-myanmar-census2024.py` inventories **all 19 sheets and every numeric column** in the demographic appendix. Its ignored `evidence/MMR_CENSUS2024_AUDIT.json` records each sheet's dimensions, numeric-cell count, column counts and labels, numeric row labels, and adoption decision. The workbook contains **17,081 numeric cells** including list/table labels; Table A-1 contains 543 numeric cells across Union, state/region and urban/rural rows. The other 17 subject tables A-2–A-18, including A-2's **477-row** district/township roster, age, fertility, marital-status and other details, are **`priority_unassessed`**. Their available columns have been inventoried, not semantically accepted or rejected as a class. No township value is silently adopted from repeated township/district names without a dated code/hierarchy crosswalk.

Table A-1 has 12 printed count fields: both sexes/male/female for field-enumerated conventional households, field-enumerated institutions, statistically estimated population in unenumerated areas, and their combined total. We adopt each field for **Union plus all 15 state/region-equivalent rows**: 12×16=192 slots. The Union Report Tables 2.2 (printed p.15) and 3.1 (p.18) additionally provide the directly enumerated total and the published one-decimal enumerated and estimated percentages: 3×16=48 slots. **Total: 15 indicators and 240 observations.** The audit program checks all 12-sex/component identities, each of the 15 urban+rural subtotals, sums of all 15 areas to the Union for every count field, and the row-by-row Excel/PDF counts and percentages.

For Yangon, Ayeyawady and Nay Pyi Taw, A-1 prints `-` rather than a numeric estimated count. Table 2.2 prints **0.0% estimated**, and the total exactly equals the enumerated sum. The nine corresponding both-sex/male/female zero values are explicitly marked **AreaData calculated** with the source-dash locator; these zeros are not treated as direct numeric A-1 cells. Other numeric values are source reported. Union 2024 combined population is 51,375,327; Chin is 373,023 with **92.2% estimated**, Yangon 7,369,989 with **0.0% estimated**, and Bago 4,453,687 with **12.9% estimated**. Percentages are published state/region values; the Union share is taken directly from the Union row, never averaged from local shares. WDI's newer national population estimate is a separate indicator and is not substituted for census geography.

## Geography, planning and remaining sources

DOP Table A-1 reports seven States, seven Regions and Nay Pyi Taw. Its rows do not include a verified current official administrative code. Source-row IDs preserve exact row identity without pretending to be GAD codes. The initial geoBoundaries reference collection has **14** 2019 ADM1 polygons and an unresolved label/edition mismatch against DOP's 15 reporting rows; all polygons were withheld. The [CSO Statistical Yearbook 2023 Part 1, Table 1.11](https://www.csostat.gov.mm/FileUpload/cso/FileDownload/SYB%202023%20%28Part-1%29.pdf) is an official location for GAD district/township unit counts as of 31 March 2023, not a verified 2024 code and boundary crosswalk; its PDF body was not acquired here. The [DOP mapping page](https://www.dop.gov.mm/en/mapping) describes census mapping, without certifying the candidate's shape join.

The acquired planning-coordination article supplies only a national reference. Region/State and township legal planning competence, effective legal instruments, the issuer's actual plan, approved budget, actual spending, implementation and official evaluation remain **unverified/uncollected**. The [DOP state/region Excel catalogue](https://www.dop.gov.mm/en/data-and-maps-category/2024-stateregion-reports-excel), and [DOP 2014 census catalogue](https://www.dop.gov.mm/en/data-and-maps-category/2014-census-data) are additional source locations. They are not proof that their local tables were inspected in this candidate. The source preflight's HDX, UNHCR, DTM, IPC, MICS, DHS, WorldPop, GHSL, SALB and WaPOR availability for Myanmar remains **not checked by country/theme/year/geography**, not zero or absent.
