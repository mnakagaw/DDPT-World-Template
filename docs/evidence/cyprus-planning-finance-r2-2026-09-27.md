## Which present authority each source concerns

The original [Municipalities Law 52(I)/2022, Appendix I](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2022_1_052.pdf) (earlier original SHA-256 `1126b2d9e32fbb418e1d928e86a9ccbab26ae7797d33cef585a5138ac177f635`, PDF pp. 99–101) establishes the present **Larnaka municipality** from former Larnaka and Livadia municipalities and Voroklini community; the present **Limassol municipality** from former Limassol and Mesa Geitonia municipalities and Tserkezoi community. [Government election guidance](https://www.gov.cy/moi-elections/en/documents/local-government-elections-2024-3/municipal-elections/general-background-71/) says **Pafos municipality remained unchanged**, while Appendix I creates separate Eastern Pafos, Akamas and Polis Chrysochous municipalities within Pafos district. These names are *municipal legal entities*. The 2021 CYSTAT locality codes, statistical districts, district local-government organisations and post-2024 municipal districts are different unit systems. No official code/date/boundary crosswalk was obtained in this subtask, even for unchanged Pafos. Accordingly, none of the material below was attached to a 2021 Census locality ID or its value.
