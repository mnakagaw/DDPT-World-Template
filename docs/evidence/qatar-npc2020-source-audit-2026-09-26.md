## Geographic and table crosswalk

The original PDF's administrative division on **PDF pp. 43–47** identifies eight municipalities and **92 Zone Nos**. XLSX Table 2 has populated rows for **87 zones**. Zone Nos **10, 11, 19, 60 and 62** are listed by the PDF but lack Table 2 population rows; all five are in Doha and remain **missing, not zero**. Table 2's 87 population/male/female rows sum to the Table 1 direct municipality rows for all eight municipalities. Table 1's municipality rows sum to the direct Qatar row, **2,846,118**. Doha is **1,186,023**, Al Rayyan **826,786**, Al Shamal **16,730**; Zone 1 Al Jasra is **132**, Zone 55 **226,747**, and Zone 98 Al Adaid **4**. Zone number is a source-reported code; official *municipality* codes and a 2020-compatible polygon edition have not been acquired. The 2015 geoBoundaries reference polygons remain **unjoined**.

The 29 indicators produce **522 local census observations**: **495 source-reported** and **27 calculated**. Every observation has a Table/sheet/row/column locator; broad ages identify a row span and calculation provenance. Parent values are direct source rows, never a subtotal from an incomplete child set. The separate WDI population series is a midyear estimate and is not harmonized to the December 2020 census; for example the site's WDI 2025 national value differs from the census value. The census methodology's household enumeration has a moving reference time, so a displayed year of 2020 does not assert that every item was observed at one instant.

## Planning and reuse

The [Ministry of Municipality's MSDP directory](https://www.mm.gov.qa/QatarMasterPlan/English/MSDP-Municipalities.aspx?panel=about) says it prepared a plan for each of eight municipalities. Its [zoning page](https://www.mm.gov.qa/QatarMasterPlan/English/msdp-zoning.aspx?panel=about) describes application to six and future extension to Al Khor/Al Wakra, so current operative coverage and amendments require a dated legal check. The site lists this strategy **only as a historical reference, body acquired, official state unverified**. It has not adopted a current complete MSDP, a revision, current zone map, municipality budget, execution or evaluation.
