## Official census universe and field decisions

The [CYSTAT-DB 2021 census API catalogue](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021) lists **46 tables in 12 directories**. `scripts/inventory-cyprus-cystat-census2021.py` archived the complete result bodies of **seven** selected matrices through their official PxWeb API: [population, sex and age `1891108E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Place%20of%20Residence/1891108E.px), housing/households/institutions by [locality `1891161E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Place%20of%20Residence/1891161E.px) and [quarter `1891164E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Place%20of%20Residence/1891164E.px), [citizenship `1891213E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Country%20of%20Citizenship%2C%20Country%20of%20Birth/1891213E.px), [education `1891515E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Education%20Attainment/1891515E.px), [labour `1891712E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Population/Population%20-%20Labour%20Force/1891712E.px), and [household size `1895114E`](https://cystatdb.cystat.gov.cy/api/v1/en/8.CYSTAT-DB/Population/Census%20of%20Population%20and%20Housing%202021/Households/Households%20-%20Household%20Size/1895114E.px). The other **39 catalogue table bodies remain location-only**, with no claim about their contents. Request JSON, response URLs, source hashes and metadata are in private `evidence/CYP_CYSTAT2021_SOURCE_INVENTORY.json`.

The seven complete bodies contain **49,964 cells**, of which **49,957 are numeric and seven are source `N.A.`**. The private `evidence/INDICATOR_INVENTORY.csv` assigns a role, indicator mapping and decision to each of **120 non-geographic source-field combinations**: 24 directly adopted at the source's main geography, eight adopted for quarter rows only, 17 age components adopted into three calculated indicators, six cross-check only, and **65 priority-unassessed**. An adopted source-field combination does not imply that all 46 tables or all its alternative sex/age classifications were semantically accepted. The seven `N.A.` values are average household size where the source records zero households. `Pitargou` has one institution resident and zero household residents, so the absent household-size row is left missing.

The acquired [nine-page final release](https://library.cystat.gov.cy/NEW/Census2021-Final_Results-EN-090824.pdf), SHA-256 `8748a4db1f53a7d11203deef6a53c126c996344b4217d789d1de99ed9d7037bd`, states that its **923,381 usual residents** cover government-controlled areas as at 1 October 2021. The five district figures are Lefkosia 350,035, Ammochostos 54,318, Larnaka 155,765, Lemesos 262,157 and Pafos 101,106. The release also reports 492,931 housing units and 357,858 households; those match the selected API totals. The 2023 preliminary announcement of 923,272 and WDI country midyear series are separate, unadopted definitions for this census indicator. No whole-island value is inferred from the five-district sum.

The importer checks each population sex/age decomposition, all five district and controlled-area totals for selected source columns, housing overlap across two tables, 18 quarter-parent groups in eight columns, citizenship/education/labour decompositions and household-size totals. Its private `evidence/CYP_CYSTAT2021_AUDIT.json` gives the exact check counts and exception codes. The adopted candidate has **516 territory records**: one country reference, one distinct census-coverage area, five districts, 410 statistical municipality/community localities and 99 quarters. It contains **27 domestic indicators and 11,804 domestic observations**: 10,556 source reported and 1,248 AreaData sums of disjoint age groups; seven of the source-reported entries have explicit `not_applicable` status. The complete source geographic ledgers remain in the comparison sets even where a field is missing. Six population-zero localities are absent from the housing table, 14 from citizenship/education/labour; missing rows are not converted into zero observations.

## Geographic and planning limits

The acquired [official GEOCODES 2015 CSV](https://www.data.gov.cy/index.php/en/dataset/821), SHA-256 `500b4e39457d7edcf33cd7cef93e2df4db26cc5ea97761b0d53f597ef9a1398f`, has 772 rows, 615 municipality/community codes and 157 quarter codes. **All 410 census locality codes and 99 quarter codes occur in this older classification**, with district-digit consistency. This proves code existence in the older ledger, not unchanged 2021 boundaries or identity with the municipalities/councils created in 2024. The [DLS INSPIRE administrative-units catalogue](https://data.gov.cy/en/dataset/542) identifies district, municipality/community and parish polygons, but its metadata gives 2016 temporal coverage and no matched 2021 extraction was acquired. The 2017 geoBoundaries reference shapes remain unjoined. There is no claim of a source-compatible interactive polygon map.

The [DTPH development-plan catalogue](https://www.gov.cy/moi-tph/documents/schedia-anaptyxis/) names Local Plans, Area Plans and Policy Statements, including a 2024 Paralimni/Agia Napa/Deryneia entry. Direct catalogue retrieval returned HTTP 403 and the linked plan PDF timed out, so the candidate records plan **location only**; exact plan area, current legal effect, plan body and maps remain unverified. The [DTPH 2026 plan-preparation notice](https://www.gov.cy/moi-tph/documents/ekponisi-topikon-schedion-leykosias-lemesoy-larnakas-kai-pafoy/) describes work on Lefkosia, Lemesos, Larnaka and Pafos local plans; it is a process location, not an approved 2026 edition. The [Ministry of Interior's district local-government-organisation page](https://www.gov.cy/moi/ypoyrgeio/domh/topiki-aytodioikisi/eparchiakoi-organismoi-aytodioikisis/) identifies bodies operating since 1 July 2024; it does not make the 2021 statistical localities current legal plan units. A full operative planning law, guidance, actual local plan bodies, budget, execution and evaluation sources still require acquisition and area-specific review.
