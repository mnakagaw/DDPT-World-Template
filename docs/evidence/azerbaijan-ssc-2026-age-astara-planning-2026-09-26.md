## Column, geography and output controls

The five XLS originals have **727 numeric columns** in the tracked register: 20 adopted completely for the 101 rows of the 2026 both-sex age sheet, three adopted for **Astara city row 325 only**, and 704 still pending row/geography review. For every one of the 101 age-sheet rows, its 19 age groups sum to the printed total. The 14 disjoint first-level SSC reporting rows sum to the national **10,262,351 persons**; each has a separately printed parent total equal to its direct child rows. These 14 include Baku city and 13 statistical economic regions. They are a source-backed statistical cohort with different legal types, not a declaration of 14 planning authorities.

The candidate has **102 territories** (country, 14 first-level reporting rows, 86 city/district rows, and the separately reported Astara city settlement), **23 domestic indicators, 2,023 domestic observations, one city document and zero adopted polygons**. The bootstrap WDI series contributes a separate 312 national records (293 observed, 19 explicit missing) and 12 indicators, so the whole dataset has 2,335 observations and 35 indicators. Two bootstrap provider shapes are withheld: no official code, legal edition and source-geography join is established. All unverified local IDs remain source-row IDs. Astara city and Astara district have separate 2024 sampled codes and different units/values.

The importer marks Astara district's registered city child as an **incomplete cover**. The normal hierarchy reaches the city and can reselect the whole district, while internal or thematic comparison never treats that one city as the whole district. The city's plan appears only on the city planning page. The district's city-specific indicator is missing; its exact reported resident population is 113,646 persons, without a child substitution.

The candidate and replay both passed `validate-country` with zero errors/warnings, built, and passed the source-pinned ten-area output verifier. The verifier checks initial/final CSV row counts and selected values across Azerbaijan, Baku, Binagadi, Nakhchivan, Garabagh, Lankaran-Astara, Astara district, East Zangezur, Salyan and Astara city; it also checks the city-only document and 14-member thematic comparison. Replayed raw receipts matched all 11 pinned SHA-256 hashes. Canonical territory and indicator arrays matched exactly; the 2,023 adopted domestic observation tuples had the same SHA-256 `2b0e0fc3de2952d66581a7113864cac540400159f623e5789c669c85fa3f1c7f`.
