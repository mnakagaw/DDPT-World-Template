# Burkina Faso planning law: selected article review (2026-09-24)

This is a source review for the **local, unpublished** AreaData Burkina Faso build. It is not a legal opinion, a complete review of all 444 articles, or proof of the current consolidated text. The 2019 census geography must not be treated as a verified crosswalk to the 2025 authorities.

## Source and method

- [Law no. 024-2025/ALT, posted scan](https://www.academiedepolice.bf/index.php/telechargement/category/25-deconcentration-et-decentralisation?download=226:code-general-des-colectivite), 114 PDF pages, SHA-256 `6a640fbaffc532df85202fe68904393966c554137ed33d96848c3cf49dd3adc2`; the original stays in the ignored project at `raw/planning/collectivities-code-2025.pdf`.
- [Legislative Assembly adoption notice](https://alt.bf/535) identifies adoption on 30 December 2025. The PDF's first page includes a promulgation decree; the legal effect of any later amendment or implementing regulation was not established.
- The scanned PDF was OCR'd locally to locate provisions. Every article in the table below was then checked against its rendered page image. OCR alone was not treated as authoritative text. Page numbers below are **PDF page / printed page**.

The [MEF PCD guide](https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/fichiers/GMPL_PCD_Version_definitive_1.pdf) and [MEF PRD guide](https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/fichiers/GMPL_PRD_Version_definitive_1.pdf) are dated December 2024. Their five-year planning process remains useful methodological context but predates this law and the [2025 administrative reform notice](https://www.presidencedufaso.bf/conseil-des-ministres-du-2-juillet-2025/) describing 17 regions and 47 provinces. AreaData's 2019 census view retains 13 regions and 45 provinces and does not assign a contemporary legal plan to each old statistical unit.

## Local plan and budget acquisition

Official procurement notices establish **preparation activity**, not an adopted PCD, full municipal budget, implementation report or evaluation. The [DGCMEF Komsilga notice](https://www.dgcmef.gov.bf/sites/default/files/2025-07/Quotidien%20N%C2%B04176.pdf) describes a 2025 consultant solicitation for a PCD financed by the commune budget; the [DGCMEF Banfora notice](https://www.dgcmef.gov.bf/sites/default/files/2025-09/Quotidien%20N%C2%B04234.pdf) similarly solicits preparation. Neither PDF is joined to a 2019 census territory as an approved plan. The source-location search has not produced a verified plan body, adopted budget, execution report or evaluation for the four representative AreaData territories (Burkina Faso, Centre, Kadiogo, Komki-Ipala).

Next: obtain the current consolidated code and implementing regulations; identify post-reform official territory IDs; obtain signed area-specific PCD/PRD, budget and execution materials from the responsible authority; then verify their territory, period, status and content before joining them to the dashboard.
