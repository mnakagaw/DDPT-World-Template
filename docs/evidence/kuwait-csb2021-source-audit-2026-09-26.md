# Kuwait CSB registration census source audit — 2026-09-26

Scope: **partial, unpublished** country candidate in ignored `generated/kuwait-areadata-20260926`. The generated source preflight had no Kuwait-specific researched locations. The [CSB 2021 registration census portal](https://census.csb.gov.kw/CensusInfo_EN) describes integration of administrative records by the Central Statistical Bureau (CSB) and Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI). The [official governorate result](https://census.csb.gov.kw/Census_Gov_EN) reports **4,385,717** nationally. It also states exclusions from its non-Kuwaiti scope (residency violators, visa offenders and entry-attribute cases). Do not equate the registration-census universe with all current residents or World Bank midyear estimates. The CSB population-series catalog separately labels the final registration-census status as 1 January 2022; the original tables are titled *2021 registration census*. The site uses period `2021` with this distinction in source metadata.

The six acquired official [census catalog categories](https://census.csb.gov.kw/CensusData_EN?CatID=1) list **118 uniquely numbered tables**: population 38, households 7, education 15, labour 48, outside labour force 3, disability 7. `KWT_CSB2021_CATALOG_INVENTORY.json` records all 118 titles, IDs, category URLs and private catalog HTML hashes. This is a **location inventory**, not verification of 118 numeric originals. **Eight** originals were acquired as pinned Excel/PDF pairs, with 16 file hashes and receipts in the ignored candidate. The other **110 table bodies and numeric fields are unassessed**. Source redistribution terms are under review, so originals and the private dataset are not committed.

## Geography, comparability and residual

Table 1's six governorate populations are **574,839; 926,170; 923,784; 566,861; 1,109,819; 279,666**. Their sum is **4,381,139**, below the published national **4,385,717** by the separately reported **4,578** people with geography *Not Stated*. That residual is a distinct reporting ID, not a seventh governorate or an allocation to another area. National values are direct Table 1 rows; they are never calculated from the six governorates. All eight tables retain their own unassigned row.

Tables 51 and 52 list 157 distinct named Area rows in the same order. Every Table 51 count equals the corresponding Table 52 all-person total. Six contiguous Table 52 blocks have **33, 17, 41, 33, 20 and 13** rows; all **nine** nationality/sex columns of each block sum exactly to its Table 1 governorate row. The candidate uses this strong table-order and nine-column reconciliation to link Areas for internal diagnosis. It explicitly records that this is **not an official code crosswalk, polygon match or legal classification**. All 157 Area IDs are source-qualified, and same-name reporting levels remain separate. The six governorates and 157 Areas have no verified official 2021/current codes or compatible polygons. The [official 2011 CSB GIS report](https://gis.csb.gov.kw/media/pdf/1_Chapter1_Administrative_Division_of_Kuwait.pdf) is historical; six geoBoundaries reference shapes were left unjoined. The site shows values and all member rows with the boundary gap.

For the 2021 census, national **1,488,716 Kuwaiti + 2,897,001 non-Kuwaiti = 4,385,717**. Table 22 national labour force is **2,570,091**; Table 26 unemployed is **23,992**, so the AreaData census-labour-force unemployment share is **0.93%** after two-decimal rounding. Table 42 national people-with-disabilities count is **55,232**. The independent 2021 WDI national midyear population in the candidate is **4,360,865**, retained under its own method and source rather than harmonized to the census. National WDI observations are never distributed to Areas.
