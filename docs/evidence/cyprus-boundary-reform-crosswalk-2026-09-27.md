## Code audit against the fixed 2021 candidate

The candidate's `CYP_2021_CENSUS_DLS_CODE_CROSSWALK.csv` lists **410** 2021 Census local codes. Every code occurs once in the pre-reform DLS `VIL_CCD` attribute; this is a code join, not a certified 1 Oct 2021 polygon equivalence. The new [410-row CYSTAT official-code crosswalk](CYP_2021_TO_2026_OFFICIAL_CODE_CROSSWALK.csv) (SHA-256 `5f261d98b99219d4c417ff6dcbd6903f9295b1e43830cf69f86127cbc4ce8f98`) joins **408 of 410** Census four-digit codes uniquely to the CYSTAT 2026 `PREVIOUS GEOGRAPHICAL CODE` column: 91 are current `MUNICIPAL QUARTER`, two are unchanged `MUNICIPALITY` (**Strovolos 1012 → 11021012; Pafos 6000 → 61016000**), and 315 are `COMMUNITY` in the statistical classification. Municipal-quarter parent municipality codes come from the current code hierarchy, not from matching names. The 91 quarter rows and two direct municipality rows do not justify summing historical 2021 observations into current 2026 values.

The remaining **Kosi 4217** (Larnaka) and **Fasli 6354** (Pafos) are in the 2021 Census and both DLS ZIPs, but have no previous-code row in the CYSTAT 2026 XLSX. The 2021 Census table publishes zero persons for each; zero does not authorise deletion from historical geography or assignment to another current authority. Across all 615 DLS previous codes, exactly 613 occur uniquely in CYSTAT 2026; these two are the complete exceptions. No formal disposition or successor code for them was found. Keep `current_entity_unresolved` for these two.

The DLS old and new ZIPs both contain the same set of 615 `VIL_CCD` attributes. **All 410 candidate polygons differ geometrically** between their 2021-published and August 2024 editions. The median symmetric-difference area divided by the larger polygon area is about `8.65e-8`; 33/410 exceed 0.1%, two exceed 1%, maximum 3.43%. Small coordinate differences may reflect editing or precision rather than legal change; the available originals cannot distinguish that. The old ZIP remains display-only; substituting the current ZIP would erase the date distinction.

## Present-day DLS polygon conflict

Using **all 613** predecessor-code rows in the CYSTAT 2026 workbook, I dissolved the relevant DLS August 2024 old-code polygons by current municipality code and compared each union with the DLS published municipality polygon. The [20-plus-nine-row spatial-check table](CYP_2026_MUNICIPAL_CODE_TO_DLS_2024_SHAPE_CHECK.csv) (SHA-256 `372615bef51333f37c10ae37b1e16209960e113d443ce3326c2033e083e5e090`) retains the constituents and intersection-over-union (IoU). Nine occupied municipality codes have no corresponding current DLS municipality polygon in this ZIP. Of the 20 operational municipality polygons, **16 have IoU above 99%**; four differ materially. `raw/boundary-followup/cluster_mismatch.json` records the intersecting old codes and areas.

| Aradippou `41020000` | IoU `0.876413333` | DLS includes Kosi `4217`, about 13.665 km² beyond the three coded members | Kosi has no 2026 previous-code row. Municipalities Law Appendix I lists Aradippou, Kellia and Troulloi, not Kosi. |
| South Nicosia–Idalion `11050000` | IoU `0.903900673` | DLS includes Agios Sozomenos `1121`, about 10.690 km² | CYSTAT 2026 gives Agios Sozomenos `12001121` as a separate `COMMUNITY`; original law's South Nicosia list does not include it. |

The 2024 DLS polygons, 2026 statistical codes and original 2022 appendix have different dates and purposes. The overlap result flags an unresolved source conflict; it does **not** prove that DLS is wrong or that the 2026 statistical code alone settles legal land jurisdiction. CYSTAT's `COMMUNITY` row is also not, by itself, proof of an elected community council: a separate Ministry council register and effective-date evidence are required. Keep service clusters and district organisations in separate entity types.

## Gate status and next action

1. **2021 Census-day shape:** Unclosed. Obtain an officially certified DLS/CYSTAT 1 Oct 2021 polygon edition or a dated change log showing that the November 2020 layer remained the applicable legal geography on Census day, including feature revisions. Current code equality and resource publish date are insufficient.
2. **Historic-to-current code relation:** 408/410 official predecessor-code links are available; Kosi and Fasli remain unresolved. A current CYSTAT clarification or later authoritative classification notice must settle those two. No historical value should be silently reassigned.
3. **Current municipality geometry:** Unclosed for Aradippou, South Nicosia, Athienou and Polis. Request a DLS/Interior authoritative explanation or corrected coded current polygon edition for the four specified disagreements. Retain both source claims and the 20-row spatial check.
4. **Current planning authority:** Pafos `61016000` and Strovolos `11021012` have direct official predecessor-code rows and DLS spatial consistency; other municipal quarters have explicit 2026 municipality parents. Attach contemporary municipal plans, budgets or audited accounts to a **new current-municipality entity**, not to the 2021 statistical locality ID. Verify each document's title, authority, period and approval separately. Community-council and district-organisation documents need their own authority IDs.
5. **District organisations:** Original law Articles 3–4 supports district-scoped, legally separate bodies with occupied-area exception. No official organisation-specific statistical code or separately versioned polygon was acquired. Do not turn the candidate's five Government-controlled 2021 Census district totals into present-day district-organisation values.
6. **Publication:** Keep the country edition `REJECT` and local. A code crosswalk and open licence do not complete the legal boundary, planning attachment, semantic coverage, 42-scenario or independent acceptance gates.
