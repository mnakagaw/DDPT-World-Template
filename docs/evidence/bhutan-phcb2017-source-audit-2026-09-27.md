## Official originals and adoption

The [National Statistics Bureau (NSB) PHCB catalogue](https://nsb.gov.bt/phcb/) lists the [2017 national report](https://nsb.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2017-PHCB-report-_national.pdf) and 20 Dzongkhag reports. `scripts/fetch-bhutan-phcb2017.py` acquired all **21 census PDFs**, the [NSB statistical geographic code publication](https://nsb.gov.bt/bhutan-standard-statistical-geograpgic-codes-2/) and the [2012 Local Government Rules and Regulations](https://www.dlgdm.gov.bt/storage/upload-documents/2021/12/16/umXB6iM4gC.pdf): 23 original PDFs, 42,218,449 bytes. The official URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `config/bhutan-phcb2017-source-manifest.json`; the saved catalogue snapshots and receipt are in the ignored project. Redistribution terms were not confirmed, so the originals are not committed.

The national report's **Table 2.1**, printed p10/PDF p29, directly gives **727,145** people in its detailed-analysis population, **380,453 male** and **346,692 female**. The immediately preceding text says **735,553** people were found on the reference day, including **8,408 non-Bhutanese/tourists in hotels** for whom detailed information was not collected. The PDF page was rendered and visually inspected. The 735,553 all-found count is a separate **national-only** indicator; it is not compared with or propagated into the 20 Dzongkhag detailed-analysis rows. WDI national midyear estimates remain a separate series.

`scripts/inventory-bhutan-phcb2017.py` hashes each district original and audits **every selected numeric row and three count columns** in Annex **Table A2.1**, *Population by Sex and Gewog/Town, Dzongkhag 2017*: 329 rows across 20 reports = 20 Dzongkhag totals, 40 Urban/Rural subtotals, 64 town/Thromde rows and 205 rural Gewog rows. Every male + female = total, Urban + Rural = Dzongkhag, town sum = Urban, and rural Gewog-row sum = Rural is checked for all three count fields. The 20 Dzongkhag totals sum to the national detailed-analysis **727,145**. The audit stores each original PDF page, source line, field values, row type and adopted status in ignored `evidence/BTN_PHCB2017_AUDIT.json`. Haa Table A2.1 PDF p41 was rendered and visually inspected: Haa 13,655, Haa Town 2,596 and Bji rural row 3,321.

The district reports contain **661 distinct numbered table headings** in the mechanical inventory. The 20 A2.1 tables have the bounded numeric audit above; the **other 641 headings are `priority_unassessed`**. Their original numeric fields, denominators, geography and definitions have not been audited or adopted. One table does not establish the absence of other Bhutan local statistics.

## Statistical geography and comparability

The candidate contains **290 reporting territories**: country, 20 Dzongkhags, 64 town/Thromde rows and 205 **rural Gewog parts**. Urban/Rural subtotals are retained in the audit only, since displaying them alongside their constituent rows would double count. Four indicators and **871 direct observations** are connected: population/male/female for all 290 areas plus the distinct national all-found count. There are no calculated parent observations. Country→Dzongkhag and Dzongkhag→town/rural-part comparisons use the exact nonoverlapping 2017 A2.1 partition. All 20 district parents and their town/rural-part children reconcile for the three selected fields.

The NSB code PDF says **December 2020, version 1.0** on its opening pages. Its 2017 census compatibility, historical code changes and legal geographic identity have not been established. Source-row ordinal IDs are explicitly internal, `official_code: null`. A census rural Gewog row does not necessarily cover the whole legal Gewog because urban towns are separate. Thromde/town labels do not by themselves establish council jurisdiction. The initial provider reference shapes were removed; **zero official 2017-compatible polygons** are joined. The site displays the no-boundary alternative and keeps source geography, planning authority and legal jurisdiction distinct.
