# Lebanon CAS LFHLCS source audit — 2026-09-26

Scope: **partial, unpublished** candidate at ignored `generated/lebanon-areadata-20260926`. Its generated `SOURCE_PREFLIGHT.md` and JSON had no Lebanon-specific lead. The [CAS LFHLCS survey catalogue](https://www.cas.gov.lb/surveys-censuses/labour-force-and-household-living-conditions-survey-2018-2019-lebanon/) and [main report](https://www.cas.gov.lb/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Labour-Force-and-Household-Living-Conditions-Survey-2018-2019.pdf) were checked before adoption. The study is a **four-round household survey conducted April 2018–March 2019**, not a census. The report's mean population estimate is referenced to **mid-2018**. Its population universe is residents of residential dwellings; the survey excludes people living in non-residential units, including refugee camps, adjacent gatherings and informal settlements. These figures cannot stand for all residents under a different population definition.

Originals and acquisition receipts remain privately under `raw/official/`; `scripts/fetch-lebanon-cas-sources.py` checks file signatures and fixed SHA-256 hashes. Reuse and redistribution terms require review. The private `evidence/LBN_CAS_SOURCE_FIELD_AUDIT.json` inventories the acquired sheet names/shapes and 35 selected geographic rows. This is **not** a completed semantic audit of all source fields.

Four local survey indicators yield **140 direct observations**: residents and households from the printed table, women and men from HL.5, each for national + eight governorates + 26 caza rows. The national published values are **4,842,500 residents** and **1,266,700 households**. Sum of eight separately rounded governorate rows is respectively **4,842,600** and **1,266,600**. The 100-person/household differences are source rounding; the dashboard keeps every published parent value directly and never substitutes a child subtotal. The two sex estimates also retain source precision/conversion details in locators. International WDI national midyear estimates remain a separate series; no subtraction or local allocation is configured.

The survey reports eight governorates and 26 caza. Beirut and Akkar each appear as both a governorate and a coextensive caza: they retain separate type-specific source IDs and observations, but their duplicated scopes are not added and their governorates stop internal subdivision. The generated 2017 geoBoundaries reference has **nine** first-level shapes. No official code, historical correspondence or period-compatible local polygon has been verified, so all local polygons were left unjoined. The top location panel explicitly reports missing verified boundaries. The two coextensive caza records are retained in the dataset but hidden from the ordinary area selector by the terminal-governorate control; this is a known selection limitation to resolve before acceptance.
