# India: Tripura planning, finance and evaluation source follow-up — 2026-09-27

## Scope and decision

West Tripura was used as a representative follow-up to the [private 2011 Census candidate](india-areadata-partial-2026-09-27.md). The five sources below are **official locations only**. The Tripura-hosted PDFs were indexed on official government domains, but direct local HTTP HEAD requests timed out on 2026-09-27; no PDF bytes, hash, visual page check or full table inventory are recorded. No source below was added to the country dataset. The Census district and its `99999` residual are historical statistical areas; neither is silently identified with a present District Panchayat, Gram Panchayat or village committee.

## Current LGD aggregate snapshot acquired

The public [LGD state-wise administrative-unit and PRI summary](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/demo/reportonStatewiseEntityDetails.do) was fetched directly with `scripts/collect-india-lgd-state-summary.py`. Its 159,695-byte private HTML snapshot has SHA-256 `c11bef2f8da1f897f178d6971e542c0746f404b88d4bb16f81cad99b6edbd163`; the source page reports generation on **27/09/2026 05:08:53 AM** and says state departments update/manage the data. The ignored receipt is `generated/india-areadata-20260927/evidence/LGD_STATE_SUMMARY_20260926T233852Z.json`. The exact table header and Tripura row show **8 districts**, **8 District Panchayats**, **1 district-level traditional body**, **23 sub-districts**, **58 development blocks**, **35 Intermediate Panchayats**, **40 intermediate traditional bodies**, **898 villages**, **607 Village Panchayats**, **587 village-level traditional bodies**, and **27 urban bodies**. These are entity-type counts, not mutually interchangeable geographies or verified plan counts.

The 2011 ORGI PCA candidate lists four Tripura districts, codes 289–292: West, South, Dhalai and North Tripura. The live LGD summary has eight current districts. This directly demonstrates why the 2011 district hierarchy cannot stand in for current legal entities. The report provides **counts only**; it supplies neither the eight current district codes/names, change events, rural/tribal/urban coverage, a 2011-to-current crosswalk nor boundary polygons. The [LGD download directory](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/demo/downloadDirectory.do) exposes entity and mapping choices but requires a CAPTCHA for generation, so no coded directory was acquired through it. The [OGD LGD catalogue](https://data.gov.in/catalog/local-government-directory-lgd) describes monthly CSV resources, but a direct request returned HTTP 503 on this run. No CAPTCHA was bypassed and no name-only join was made.
