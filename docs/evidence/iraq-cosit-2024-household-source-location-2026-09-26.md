# Iraq COSIT 2024 census: catalogue and two household tables

Checked 2026-09-26 in the separate, ignored `generated/iraq-areadata-20260926-r2` candidate. This is source-location and partial-adaptation evidence, not independent acceptance.

The prior [government open-data record](https://nogp.gov.iq/DatasetDetails.aspx?id=17) supplied the 2024 final governorate summary workbook; its acquisition and five-field audit are recorded in [the earlier source-location note](iraq-census-source-location-2026-09-26.md). This r2 candidate retains that source without altering the saved original project.

- [COSIT census table catalogue](https://cosit.gov.iq/ar/1234-2019-09-05-07-53-20): 30 distinct 2024 table PDFs listed. Seven originals were acquired with URL, status, byte count and SHA-256 in `raw/cosit-census-2024-catalogue.json`; the other 23 were identified but not requested. Catalogue HTML and originals stay outside Git pending reuse-terms review.
- [Electricity sources by governorate](https://cosit.gov.iq/images/census2024/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1.pdf): two pages, SHA-256 `cd385b3544553810424bcf2a9d9e22d32995a22172f57b04aed7a6d4e76e8dd7`. Page 1 has seven count fields and page 2 six percentage fields for 18 governorates plus Iraq. The national count of *regular responding households* is 7,757,742; 7,634,417 reported the public grid (98.41%). Electricity-source categories overlap and must not be summed as a household partition.
- [Primary domestic water used at home by governorate](https://cosit.gov.iq/images/census2024/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A1.pdf): two pages, SHA-256 `4c2766f98a629535a0349b4db6b91b8a4c4edec0d8bc7be388bce9881be80ab5`. Page 1 has ten count fields and page 2 ten percentage fields. Its national denominator is 8,054,385 households, different from electricity; 6,734,842 report piped water inside the dwelling as their primary domestic-water source (83.6%). This is **not** a drinking-water or safe-water measure.
- The two PDFs contain 627 checked numeric cells in their governorate and national rows. Six indicators, 114 observations, were adopted after visual row-order review, national-by-governorate summation, water-category partition and published-percentage checks. The remaining fields and decisions are retained in the ignored `evidence/COSIT_2024_HOUSEHOLD_FIELDS.csv` and `...IMPORT_AUDIT.json`. The five other acquired PDFs (disability, educational attainment, education attendance, age/sex, drinking-water source) remain unadopted; the age/sex PDF has 268 pages and is not a reviewed 268-page dataset.
- The manual source-row crosswalk reaches the 18 governorate names in the pre-existing 2024 summary candidate. Its geoBoundaries 2022 shapes are reference geometry matched by name, without official 2024 administrative-code or boundary equivalence. The candidate must not be described as a certified census geography or released as accepted. Planning law, actual plans, budgets, implementation and evaluation remain uncollected.

Rebuild with `scripts/replay-iraq-census-2024-households.py --source-project <read-only-r1> --out <new-worktree-directory>`. A fresh 2026-09-26 replay acquired seven PDFs, passed validation/build/output verification and matched the working r2 candidate's territories (19), indicators (23 including international series), observations (521 including 209 domestic) and boundaries exactly. The original saved candidate is read-only.
