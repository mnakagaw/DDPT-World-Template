## Field, geography and indicator decisions

The eCensus pivot has reference columns 2020-12-12, 2021-12-31, 2023-01-01, 2024-01-01, 2025-01-01 and 2026-01-01. **Only 2020-12-12** cells were adopted. Its English `RESIDENTIAL_STATUS` label corresponds to Arabic `الجنسية - عماني أو وافد` (Omani/expatriate nationality), as checked in the bilingual metadata and 130-row bilingual pivot. This is an electronic census based on administrative records, not a household sample survey or an annual WDI population estimate. Other eCensus dimensions and products were inventoried as metadata leads, not adopted numerical indicators.

The 2020 pivot has national Omani **2,731,456**, expatriate **1,739,692** and source grand total **4,471,148**. For 11 governorates and 61 wilayats, Omani and expatriate source counts sum exactly to their parent and national counts. Local totals are explicitly marked calculated from those two same-date source cells, never from a different geography or WDI series. The adopted scope is **3 indicators × 73 territories = 219 domestic observations**: country + 11 governors + 61 wilayats. The full candidate has 73 territories, 15 indicators, 531 observations (including separate national WDI series), one national plan document and no accepted polygons.

The 2025 MOI directory lists 63 wilayats. `ALJABAL ALAKDAR` and `SINAW` each have null Omani and expatriate cells at the 2020 eCensus reference date, so they remain in the 130-row source crosswalk and are **not represented as zero or asserted to be 2020 units**. Four unassigned `Unknown` exception rows also remain source-only; none contributes a 2020 residual. Arabic name matching provides a reproducible bridge to the MOI list, but neither MOI 2025 IDs nor the NCSI feature codes certify 2020 legal boundaries. Seven incomplete bootstrap ADM1 reference polygons were withheld; no statistical color map is claimed. Internal comparisons use the source's own complete 11/61 reporting hierarchy as tables.
