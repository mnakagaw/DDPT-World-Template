The seven first-level reported populations are Jerusalem **1,252,940**, Northern **1,519,660**, Haifa **1,104,670**, Central **2,319,540**, Tel Aviv **1,502,610**, Southern **1,420,360**, and Judea and Samaria Area **481,940**. These sum exactly to the published nationwide **9,601,720**, at the source's rounded-to-tens precision. Other adopted percentages and median ages are direct reported values and are neither summed nor averaged into parent values. The workbook refers to April 2022; the [CBS Statistical Abstract Table 2.17](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2024/2.shnatonpopulation/st02_17.pdf) identifies the census reference date as 2 April 2022 and, in footnote 8, defines Judea and Samaria Area here as **Israeli localities**. Its 481,940 is not an all-resident population for that geographic area, nor does this dataset settle territorial status. The dashboard uses one CBS first-level **reporting cohort** for comparison while retaining the distinct area subtype and source-scope note.

The initial geoBoundaries reference has six **2006** district shapes and is not code/scope equivalent to the seven CBS 2022 rows. None of its polygons is joined to the local observations. Current official district/local-authority/statistical-area geometry and code histories remain unverified. CBS [statistical-area terminology](https://www.cbs.gov.il/en/Pages/All-Terms.aspx?k=Statistical+area) describes census statistical areas within localities; their existence alone does not establish a legal planning unit. WDI national population is a separate midyear international series; no raw subtraction, local allocation or automatic semantic harmonization is made.
