The [UNSD census-date listing](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/census/censusdates) used by the bootstrap says 31 December 2021, while the acquired Table 1 prints the **2021 project label** but no precise extraction date. The dataset therefore uses the period `2021` and does not assert that a specific Table 1 cell is a 1 January 2022 bulletin value. The [official census homepage](https://census.csb.gov.kw/index_EN) displayed the different headline `4,385,291` at review time; the acquired final Table 1 and official governorate detail both say `4,385,717`. This candidate pins Table 1 as its source and records the homepage discrepancy rather than blending the two.

## Field and geographic controls

Table 1's **nine directly printed fields** are Kuwaiti male/female/total, non-Kuwaiti male/female/total, and all-nationalities male/female/total. Each of eight reporting rows satisfies male + female = its nationality total and Kuwaiti + non-Kuwaiti = its all-nationalities value. For each field, the six governorates plus nonterritorial `Not Stated` equal the source national row. For all-population total: six governorates **4,381,139**, `Not Stated` **4,578**, national **4,385,717**. The national count is an independently printed source row, never computed as six-governorate coverage. All eight rows and nine columns are retained in `evidence/KWT_CSB_2021_TABLE1_ROWS.csv`; every field decision is in `evidence/INDICATOR_INVENTORY.csv`.

Table 51's 157 named values sum to 4,381,139, and adding its nonterritorial `Not Stated` 4,578 yields 4,385,717. Contiguous row-block sums match the six Table 1 governorate totals at Excel rows 44, 61, 102, 135, 155 and 168, but the acquired table does **not** print an official code or parent key for each area. Arithmetic and row order alone are not used as an official geographic crosswalk. `evidence/KWT_CSB_2021_TABLE51_ROWS.csv` preserves every raw area row without publishing it as a joined indicator.

The six bootstrap geoBoundaries shapes refer to 2017 provider geometry. No 2021 official code/boundary equivalence has been established, so the candidate has **zero adopted polygons**. The six CSB 2021 governorates have source-label IDs only. The administrative governorate, planning authority and finer statistical area are distinct until official legal and code evidence establishes their relationship.
