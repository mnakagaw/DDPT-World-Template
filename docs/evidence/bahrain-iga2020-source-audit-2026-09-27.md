## Official source universe and adoption

The [Bahrain Open Data Census theme catalogue](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/?refine.theme=Census) and its [API](https://www.data.gov.bh/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets?limit=100&refine=theme%3ACensus) exposed **45 datasets and 2,602 records** when acquired. `scripts/inventory-bahrain-census2020.py` saved all result pages with URL and SHA-256, checked catalog/API counts and duplicate rows, and inventoried **45 numeric columns, 2,598 numeric cells and four numeric nulls**. Twenty datasets have a governorate dimension. The private `evidence/BHR_CENSUS2020_SOURCE_INVENTORY.json` lists every dataset, field, row count, source URL, acquired page and hash. `evidence/BHR_CENSUS2020_FIELD_DISPOSITION.json` records one decision for every numeric field: eight selectively adopted tables with other dimensions unassessed, seven cross-check-only tables, three excluded building tables and **27 priority-unassessed tables**. A numeric-column inventory is not a semantic acceptance of every cell.

The [iGA article on the 2020 census](https://www.iga.gov.bh/en/article/al-qaed-highlights-igas-experience-in-producing-statistics-through-administrative-records-meeting-held-with-saudi-arabias-general-authority-for-statistics) describes administrative-record production. The selected [population by governorate/nationality/sex table](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/dataset/population-by-governorate-nationality-and-sex-census-2020/) has 16 mutually exclusive cells: Capital **548,345**, Muharraq **268,106**, Northern **379,637**, Southern **305,547**, total **1,501,635**. Each nationality/sex group agrees with the 64-cell [detailed nationality table](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/dataset/population-by-governorate-nationality-groups-and-sex-census-2020/); the national sum agrees with the separate religion table. The importer pins the catalog hash `32d387cfc741d3d97fe72b3a4ba70a6474c6a9a3660cc28def16c1631c00eb4c` and the hashes of all consumed API pages.

Eight selected source tables supply **18 census indicators** for 2020. These cover population/sex/nationality, private and collective households, persons in private households, housing units, enrolled population aged 3+, economically active/unemployed people aged 15+, and population aged 15+. All parent and subgroup sums are explicit `AreaData` calculations; single-cell governorate values remain source reported. The dashboard holds **90 census observations** (five areas × 18 indicators). It does not convert unemployed counts to a rate or persons in private households to total population. Independent cross-checks found private households **228,972** in four iGA representations; all housing units **387,126** in two tables; school enrolled **309,557** in both demographic and stage tables; economically active **875,558** equal to employed plus unemployed; and aged-15+ **1,199,362** in the marital and labour tables. These checks cover the selected comparisons, not all Census-theme fields.

The separate [iGA population by governorate/nationality/sex annual table](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/dataset/02-population-by-governorate-nationality-sex/) has 320 archived records. Six complete June cohorts for 2020–2025 supply three indicators and **90 observations** over national and four governorates. The June 2020 complete sum is **1,472,204** and June 2025 **1,603,260**. The dataset metadata does not state its statistical method; the candidate labels it an official June portal series, not the 2020 census or WDI midyear series. The separate [area-by-governorate series](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/dataset/02-area-by-governorate-2023/) has 79 acquired records, and its four 2024 rows supply **four source-reported km² observations**: Capital 79.23, Muharraq 74.10, Northern 145.69 and Southern 488.77. No area value is presented as a matched polygon or census density denominator. Total domestic adoption: **22 indicators, 184 observations**.

The three 2020 building tables were **not adopted**. Building current usage reports Muharraq **50,560** and Southern **54,770**; building type and ownership each report Muharraq **54,770** and Southern **50,560**. They all total **274,733** nationally, which does not resolve the governorate swap. Building type and ownership each also contain a `#REF!` governorate row with a null building count. Seek an iGA correction and provenance before any governorate building comparison. The 27 other numeric fields remain `priority_unassessed`, including potentially useful disability, literacy, household average, vehicle, education and working-population classifications; no absent/zero conclusion is made from their nonadoption.

## Territory identity and planning evidence

The adopted iGA sources use the same four post-2014 English/Arabic governorate names. The [official area series](https://www.data.gov.bh/explore/dataset/02-area-by-governorate-2023/) describes redivision from five to four governorates in 2014. Official 2020 codes and source-compatible polygons were not acquired. The generator's 2017 geoBoundaries ADM1 reference shapes are **unjoined**; the local site says there is no verified boundary and uses the named hierarchy/list instead. Governorates are statistical diagnosis areas here; a governorate label alone does not establish the statutory author of a plan.

The [1994 Urban Planning Law](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/L0294), [2022 amendment](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/L3222), [2023 zoning decision](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/RCAB9323) and [UPDA 2023 manual catalogue](https://planning.bh/en/urban_planning_manual.html) were acquired. The linked [83-page UPDA procedure manual](https://planning.bh/pdf/uppm/Urban%20Planning%20and%20Development%20Authority%20Procedures%20Manual_English%202023.pdf) was locally acquired and text-extracted. Pages 5 and 9–10 distinguish UPDA, competent municipalities and planning applications. It is national guidance, not a completed governorate plan. The acquired six-page [Capital zoning decision and maps PDF](https://planning.bh/pdf/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Zoning%20and%20Construction%20Regulations/Capital%20Zones%20Maps%20-%20%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B7%20%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%81%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%85%D8%A9.pdf) shows Decision 15/2017, Official Gazette issue 3301, 16 February 2017, on page 1. It establishes the historical decision at that date; current map applicability and subsequent changes are unverified. The site records `body_acquired` and current institutional state `unverified`, with the 2017 decision explicitly described. No municipal budget, execution, evaluation or complete current plan is inferred.

The [portal FAQ](https://www.data.gov.bh/pages/faq/) describes open reuse of portal data; source-specific terms and redistribution of other acquired legal/manual/PDF originals still need review. No raw API pages/PDFs are committed or hosted. The full 45-dataset semantic review, lower-area codes/statistics, dated official polygons, annual-population method note, building discrepancy and current planning/fiscal bodies remain next gates.
