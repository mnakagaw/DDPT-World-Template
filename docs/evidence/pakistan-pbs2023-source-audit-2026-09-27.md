Each source has 11 numerical columns (B–L): area, 2023 population, male, female, transgender, sex ratio, density, urban proportion, household size, 2017 population and 2017–23 annual growth. All **19,899 populated numerical cells** in the 737 named Total/Rural/Urban reporting triplets were counted, with blank cells kept blank. The raw workbook also includes lower tehsil, taluka and sub-division rows. The indicator inventory records every file, numerical column and reporting class; only the six 2023 direct-count combinations (total population, male, female, transgender, rural population, urban population) at the Table 1 scope, four provinces and 136 district levels were adopted. The other numerical fields and all lower-unit rows remain `priority_unassessed`. No rate was calculated by averaging or by applying the Table 1 count as a denominator to a later table.

The source national Table 1 population is **241,499,431** (`Sheet1!C5`); its four province rows and Islamabad row sum exactly to that number. It also reports rural 147,614,729 and urban 93,884,702. The acquired Table 1 national workbook does **not** contain Azad Jammu & Kashmir or Gilgit-Baltistan reporting rows, although [PBS GIS](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/gis/) lists those separately in its census frame. Therefore the Table 1 national count is attached to an explicit **four-province-plus-ICT reporting-scope node**, separate from the Pakistan country root and WDI midyear population. The [PBS national report](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/sites/default/files/population/2023/reports/National%20Census%20Report-2023.pdf) describes the four-province/ICT district framework. Table 1's own note in national workbook rows 29, 31 and 33 says it includes headcounts mainly from restricted areas without demographic/housing detail, while Table 4 onward uses a detailed-characteristics population base. Those bases are not silently equated.

Mechanical checks found **3,684** exact Total=Rural+Urban comparisons across five count columns, **2,210** exact population=male+female+transgender row comparisons, **15** five-region-to-Table 1 national comparisons, **75** district-to-region comparisons and **75** national-vs-region-file comparisons. This is preserved as a source anomaly, **not corrected or adopted as a tehsil observation**. All 136 district and four province direct rows passed the checks used for adoption. Reported zeros such as Bajaur district urban `C25=0` and Lahore district rural `C264=0` remain observed zeros.

## Law, plans, budgets and acquisition stage

Official locations were verified for [Punjab's 2025 Local Government Act](https://lgcd.punjab.gov.pk/governing_laws), the [KP Local Government Act](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/act/the-khyber-pakhtunkhwa-local-government-act-2013/) and [2022 Tehsil rules](https://kpcode.kp.gov.pk/uploads/Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa_CityTehsil_Local_Government_Rules_of_Business_2022.pdf), [Sindh's Act section 76](https://www.sindhlaws.gov.pk/setup/publications_SindhCode/PUB-NEW-18-000156.pdf), [Balochistan's Act sections 86 and 94–95](https://balochistan.gov.pk/local-government-and-rural-development-downloads/acts/) and the [ICT Act sections 48–49](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator2cd0fb199558bc5fb239d66d51d21e27.pdf). They describe different councils/Tehsil bodies and plan/budget powers. An attempted local transfer of the Punjab PDF was interrupted before completion; the incomplete file was removed. **No legal PDF body is recorded as acquired locally** in this candidate, and later amendments and current applicability remain to be checked. The [federal PSDP FY 2026–27 catalogue](https://www.pc.gov.pk/web/psdp) is a verified national programme/expenditure location; it is not a local council plan or evidence of implementation for a selected 2023 census district.

The planning page presents law and census links as references with their acquisition stage; it shows **no acquired local plan, budget, expenditure or evaluation** for the selected area. A 2023 census district is a statistical reporting unit and is not presumed to be a current local council or legal planning unit. Common international source locations in the generated preflight remain availability-unchecked for the exact Pakistan local themes, periods and granularities; the WDI national estimates were acquired separately by the generator.
