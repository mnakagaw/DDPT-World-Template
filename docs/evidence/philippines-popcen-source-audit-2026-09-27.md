# Philippines 2024 POPCEN and local-planning source audit — 2026-09-27 JST

**Result: partial local candidate, unpublished, independent `ACCEPT` pending.** The generated project `generated/philippines-areadata-20260927` and its 36 acquired PDF/XLSX originals remain outside Git. This audit covers the acquired [PSA 2024 POPCEN release](https://psa.gov.ph/content/2024-census-population-popcen-population-counts-declared-official-president), [PSGC as of 30 June 2024](https://psa.gov.ph/content/second-quarter-2024-psgc-updates-creation-negros-island-region-and-correction-names-two), the later Q4 code edition, and selected national/Quezon City planning materials. It is not an audit of every 2024 census theme or every Philippine local plan.

## Planning law, guide and selected actual materials

[Republic Act No. 7160](https://officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) section 106 establishes a comprehensive multisectoral development plan for each local government unit, initiated by its development council and approved by its sanggunian. Sections 109 and 114 distinguish investment functions and relation to regional planning. A region is a statistical/coordination level here, and the census SGA subtotal is not itself an LGU. The Official Gazette page was read online but not locally archived; amended-law and full implementing-rule review is still pending. The DILG Region I [CDP Facilitator's Toolkit](https://region1.dilg.gov.ph/images/Transparency/BOOKS/CDP-TOOLKIT-FINAL%20AS%20OF%20APRIL%208%202022.pdf) PDF body has **502 pages**; cover and selected contents were checked. Its full forms, current adoption and application to different LGU types are not audited. The PDF restricts redistribution, so its original remains only in ignored raw storage.
