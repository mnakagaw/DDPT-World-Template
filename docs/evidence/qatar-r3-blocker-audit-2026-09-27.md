## Boundary change: legal original acquired

The [Al Meezan legal record for Ministerial Resolution 109/2024](https://almeezan.qa/LawPage.aspx?id=9617&language=ar) and its [official Gazette issue 9](https://www.almeezan.qa/PDF/2024/9.pdf) identify Article 1 as a change to **Al Wakra Municipality** according to an attached map and coordinates. Article 3 makes it effective on issue, 30 May 2024; the Gazette was published 10 July 2024, page 219. The separate [Gazette-page original](https://almeezan.qa/ClarificationsNoteDetails.aspx?id=19975&language=ar) and [attached map image](https://almeezan.qa/ClarificationsNoteDetails.aspx?id=20065&language=ar) were acquired and visually inspected, with hashes in the R3 legal raw directory. The [2011 baseline Resolution 68](https://almeezan.qa/LawPage.aspx?id=3837&language=ar) was acquired too.

The [current MunicipalityAT GIS layer](https://services.gisqatar.org.qa/server/rest/services/Vector/MunicipalityAT/MapServer/0) returned eight polygons and code attributes. Comparing the same municipality codes against the eight display-only polygons dissolved from [GIS Census_Zone_2020](https://services.gisqatar.org.qa/server/rest/services/Vector/Census_Zone/MapServer/0) gives a 0.062095066 symmetric-difference/union fraction for Al Wakra. This is a shape diagnostic in longitude/latitude coordinates, **not a geodesic area or proof that every difference came from the 2024 amendment**. The legal map is an image, not a machine-readable legal boundary; the current GIS feature's `STARTDATE` is metadata, not an established legal effective date. The current GIS polygon has not been matched coordinate-by-coordinate to the legal annex. Keep 2020 census values on 2020 geography; do not transfer them to the current legal geography. `evidence/QAT_R3_BOUNDARY_RECONCILIATION.json` records all eight comparisons and raw-source hashes.
