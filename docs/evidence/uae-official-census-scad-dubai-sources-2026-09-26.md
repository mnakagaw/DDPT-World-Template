## Geography, recent data and planning limits

FCSC's seven printed 2005 emirate names map one-to-one by name to the seven `geoBoundaries` gbOpen ADM1 reference shapes dated 2017. `Ras Al-Khaimah`/`Ras al-Khaimah` and `Umm Al-Quwain`/`Umm al-Quwain` were explicitly paired. These are **provider** shapes and IDs, not official UAE codes or certified 2005/2024 legal boundaries. Official code and boundary edition, island/enclave treatment, and internal municipality/region hierarchy remain unverified. SCAD's 2024 Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra region counts sum to 4,135,985 but are not assigned to unsupported map polygons. The SCAD Abu Dhabi register series and the older FCSC census series remain separate indicators, as does national WDI midyear population.

The [official UAE government description of seven local governments](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/the-uae-government/the-local-governments-of-the-seven-emirates) confirms different local institutions and responsibilities but supplies no official code/boundary register. [Dubai Municipality's plan page](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/planning-and-construction/dubai-2040-urban-master-plan/) describes its Emirate-specific role. The two Dubai documents are attached to **Dubai**, never to the country or other emirates. No budget authorization, actual expenditure, implementation result or official evaluation is inferred from the plan's targets.

Two more useful current local statistical locations were identified without acquired data: [UAE.Stat](https://uaestat.fcsc.gov.ae/en) lists births by emirate, gender and citizenship (`FCSA:DF_BIRTHS(1.9.0)`), and the [Dubai 2024 population bulletin](https://www.dsc.gov.ae/Publication/Population%20Bulletin%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai%20-%202024.pdf) is indexed by the official Dubai statistics site. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403 for both in this environment; no number from either was adopted. Their detailed export, terms, period and definitions need independent inspection. Other emirates' 2024 values and plans/budgets remain **not collected**, not zero or nonexistent. Common international subnational candidates in the generated preflight were not individually availability-checked in this pass.
